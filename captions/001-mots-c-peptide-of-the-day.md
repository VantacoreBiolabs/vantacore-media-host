PEPTIDE OF THE DAY: MOTS-c 🧬

MOTS-c is a mitochondria-derived peptide being studied for its role in cellular energy signaling, metabolic health, and exercise-response pathways.

Why it matters: mitochondria help turn nutrients into usable energy. Researchers are exploring how signaling peptides such as MOTS-c may influence the way cells respond to metabolic stress.

Research is ongoing, and MOTS-c is not FDA-approved to treat disease. This post is for education—not medical advice.
