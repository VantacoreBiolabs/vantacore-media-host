RESEARCH COMPOUND OF THE DAY: SLU-PP-332 🧬

First, an important correction: SLU-PP-332 is not a peptide. It is a synthetic small molecule that activates estrogen-related receptors ERRα, ERRβ and ERRγ—nuclear receptors involved in cellular energy metabolism.

In cell and mouse studies, researchers reported activation of aerobic-exercise-related gene programs, increased oxidative muscle characteristics, stronger mitochondrial and fatty-acid-metabolism signaling, and improved endurance or energy expenditure.

You may see it called “exercise in a bottle” online, but that nickname goes far beyond the evidence. These findings are preclinical. SLU-PP-332 is not approved for human use, and established human safety, effectiveness and dosing data do not exist.
