COA CHECK: 5 THINGS TO VERIFY 🔬

A Certificate of Analysis can provide useful evidence—but seeing “99.9%” at the top of a page should never end the evaluation.

Before trusting a COA, ask:

1️⃣ Can the testing laboratory be independently verified?
2️⃣ Does the lot or sample number match the material being represented?
3️⃣ Which methods were used? HPLC can estimate purity, while mass spectrometry can help confirm identity.
4️⃣ Is the testing date current and connected to that specific batch?
5️⃣ What was not tested?

A purity result alone does not establish sterility, endotoxin safety, accurate fill weight, or proper handling. Strong research literacy means checking what the report proves—and what it does not.
