HALF-LIFE EXPLAINED ⏳

A common misconception is that a compound is “gone” after one half-life. Half-life only means its measured amount has fallen by about half.

Starting at 100%:
1 half-life ≈ 50%
2 half-lives ≈ 25%
3 half-lives ≈ 12.5%
4 half-lives ≈ 6.25%
5 half-lives ≈ 3%

This is a simplified model. Actual kinetics can differ because absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination, route and formulation all matter. Half-life is a research concept—not a dosing instruction.
