STORAGE MYTH VS FACT ❄️

MYTH: If a research material stays cold, repeated temperature changes do not matter.

FACT: Freeze–thaw cycles can matter. For many temperature-sensitive research materials, repeated warming and cooling may promote condensation, aggregation or degradation—potentially changing sample integrity and research results.

There is no universal storage rule for every peptide or compound. Stability depends on factors such as formulation, physical state, container, light exposure and validated handling conditions.

Good research practice:
• Follow compound-specific documentation.
• Minimize unnecessary temperature cycling.
• Record storage conditions and handling events.
• Do not assume “still cold” means “unchanged.”

Storage is part of data quality.
