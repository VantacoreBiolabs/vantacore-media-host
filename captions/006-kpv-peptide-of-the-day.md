PEPTIDE OF THE DAY: KPV 🧬

KPV stands for lysine–proline–valine. It is a tripeptide—the three-amino-acid C-terminal fragment of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH).

What makes it interesting to researchers? Laboratory studies have examined KPV in inflammatory-signaling models. A 2008 study reported that KPV could be transported through the PepT1 peptide transporter in immune and intestinal epithelial cells, while mouse experiments explored its effects in chemically induced colitis models.

That does not make KPV a proven treatment. The commonly discussed findings are preclinical, and established human safety, effectiveness and dosing data are lacking.

The useful takeaway: understand what the research model showed—and do not promote an early finding as a human result.
