TESAMORELIN vs SERMORELIN vs CJC-1295 vs IPAMORELIN vs HGH 🧬

These compounds are often grouped together, but they do not enter the growth-hormone pathway in the same way.

TESAMORELIN
A modified GHRH analog that signals the pituitary to produce and release endogenous growth hormone. It has a specific approved medical indication; that approval should not be generalized to unrelated goals.

SERMORELIN
The 1–29 fragment of GHRH. It also signals the pituitary, but its structure, evidence base and regulatory status differ from tesamorelin.

CJC-1295
A longer-acting GHRH analog studied for sustained GH and IGF-1 signaling. It remains investigational and is not interchangeable with sermorelin or tesamorelin.

IPAMORELIN
A growth-hormone secretagogue that activates the ghrelin receptor, or GHSR. This is a different receptor pathway from GHRH analogs.

HGH / SOMATROPIN
Exogenous growth hormone supplied directly. It does not ask the pituitary to release GH.

Which is “best”? There is no universal answer. A legitimate decision depends on diagnosis, clinical evidence, regulatory status, medical history, glucose metabolism, IGF-1 monitoring and individual risk—not internet rankings.

This comparison is educational and not a protocol.
