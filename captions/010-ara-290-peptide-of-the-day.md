PEPTIDE OF THE DAY: ARA-290 (CIBINETIDE) 🧬

ARA-290 is an investigational 11-amino-acid peptide engineered from the helix-B surface of erythropoietin, or EPO.

Its design goal is unusual: engage the EPOR/CD131 complex—often called the innate repair receptor—without activating the classical EPO pathway responsible for stimulating red-blood-cell production.

Researchers have explored this signaling in tissue-protection and inflammation models. Small, short-duration human trials have also examined ARA-290 in people with sarcoidosis-associated small-fiber neuropathy and neuropathic symptoms related to type 2 diabetes. Some studies reported changes in symptom measures or corneal nerve-fiber endpoints.

Those findings are research signals, not clinical proof. The studies were limited in size and duration, ARA-290 is not an approved treatment, and long-term human safety and effectiveness have not been established.
