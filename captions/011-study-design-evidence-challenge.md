RESEARCH LITERACY CHALLENGE 🔬

Which study can test human effectiveness most directly?

A — CELL STUDY
Useful for investigating mechanisms in a tightly controlled laboratory system—but cells in a dish cannot reproduce an entire human body.

B — ANIMAL STUDY
Can explore whole-body biology, exposure and safety signals before human trials. Results may not translate directly to people.

C — OBSERVATIONAL HUMAN STUDY
Can identify real-world patterns and associations. Without random assignment, other differences between groups may influence the result.

D — RANDOMIZED CONTROLLED HUMAN TRIAL
When well designed and appropriate, random assignment and a comparison group help reduce bias and test whether an intervention caused a difference.

Best answer: D—but study design is only the beginning. Sample size, controls, outcome selection, missing data, replication and relevance all affect how much confidence a result deserves.

Every design has value because every design answers a different question.
