PEPTIDE OF THE DAY: LL-37 🧬

LL-37 is a naturally occurring 37-amino-acid human host-defense peptide. It is released from the precursor protein hCAP18 and appears at epithelial barriers and in immune cells such as neutrophils.

Why do researchers study it?

🔬 MEMBRANE INTERACTIONS
Its positively charged, helical structure can interact with certain microbial membranes in laboratory systems.

🛡️ IMMUNE SIGNALING
LL-37 can influence chemotaxis, inflammatory signaling and how immune cells communicate.

🧱 BARRIER AND REPAIR BIOLOGY
Cell and animal studies have explored its roles in epithelial migration, angiogenesis and wound-response pathways.

Important context: LL-37 is not simply an “immune booster.” Its effects vary with concentration, tissue environment and disease state. The same signaling can be protective in one setting and contribute to excessive inflammation in another.

Most proposed uses remain experimental. FDA has cited insufficient safety information and potential safety concerns for compounded LL-37. Human safety, effectiveness and dosing have not been established.
