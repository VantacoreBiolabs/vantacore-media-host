LIGHT EXPOSURE CAN CHANGE SAMPLE STABILITY 💡

Light is not just a storage detail. For photosensitive materials, exposure may trigger chemical changes that alter identity, strength or purity—even when the sample still looks normal.

What determines risk?

• Wavelength: ultraviolet and visible light can affect materials differently.
• Intensity and time: exposure conditions matter together.
• Formulation: the same active material may behave differently in a solution versus a dry state.
• Packaging: clear, amber and opaque containers provide different degrees of protection.

Amber glass can reduce some light exposure, but it is not a universal guarantee. Photostability is compound- and formulation-specific, so validated documentation should determine the appropriate container and handling conditions.

Important: normal color and clarity do not prove that a light-exposed sample remains stable. Appearance is an observation—not a stability test.
