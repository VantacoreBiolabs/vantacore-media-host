pH IS PART OF SAMPLE STABILITY 🧪

pH is more than a number on a label. In peptide and protein research, it can influence molecular charge, solubility, structure, aggregation and the rate of certain chemical-degradation pathways.

That does not mean “neutral is always best.”

The most stable pH range depends on the specific molecule, formulation, concentration, excipients, temperature and time. A condition that supports one material may destabilize another.

Important research lessons:

• Never assume there is one ideal pH for every compound.
• Use compound- and formulation-specific stability data.
• Treat unexpected precipitation, cloudiness or color as observations that require investigation.
• Remember that normal appearance does not prove identity, purity or stability.

Validated analytical testing—not visual inspection or guesswork—determines whether a sample remains within specification.

This is laboratory education, not a mixing or administration guide.
