MYTH vs FACT: ASSOCIATION AND CAUSATION 🔍

MYTH: If two things occur together, one must have caused the other.

FACT: An association is a pattern worth investigating—but it does not establish cause and effect by itself.

Other explanations may include:

CONFOUNDING
A third factor influences both variables and creates or distorts the observed relationship.

REVERSE CAUSATION
The proposed outcome may actually influence the proposed cause.

BIAS OR CHANCE
Study design, measurement, selection or random variation may contribute to the result.

Confidence in a causal explanation grows when the evidence has appropriate timing, strong controls, careful design, consistency, replication and biological plausibility.

Association is not useless. It can generate an important hypothesis. The next question is: What else could explain the result?
