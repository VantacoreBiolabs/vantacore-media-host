PEPTIDE OF THE DAY: ELAMIPRETIDE (SS-31) 🧬

Elamipretide—also known as SS-31—is a synthetic, mitochondria-targeting tetrapeptide.

WHY IS IT DIFFERENT?
Research shows that elamipretide localizes to the inner mitochondrial membrane and interacts with cardiolipin, a phospholipid important to mitochondrial cristae structure and energy-system organization. Laboratory and preclinical studies have examined how that interaction may influence mitochondrial structure, electron transport and cellular energy biology.

REGULATORY REALITY
On September 19, 2025, FDA granted accelerated approval to Forzinity (elamipretide) for Barth syndrome in patients weighing at least 30 kg. The approval was based on improved knee-extensor muscle strength—a measure considered reasonably likely to predict patient benefit—and requires a confirmatory post-approval trial.

That narrow approval does not establish elamipretide for general anti-aging, wellness, athletic-performance or mitochondrial “optimization” uses. Evidence must be interpreted by indication, population and outcome.
