HUMIDITY IS A STABILITY VARIABLE 💧

Storage discussions often focus on temperature and light, but moisture exposure can matter too.

Depending on the material, formulation and packaging, humidity may influence:

• CHEMICAL STABILITY — Water can participate in degradation pathways such as hydrolysis.
• PHYSICAL FORM — Moisture may affect crystallinity, aggregation, clumping or other material properties.
• PACKAGING PERFORMANCE — Protection depends on the container-closure system and its moisture barrier.

A sample can still look dry while containing absorbed moisture. Appearance alone does not establish stability.

ICH stability guidance evaluates environmental factors—including temperature and humidity—under defined conditions. The relevant conditions depend on the material, formulation and proposed packaging; there is no single humidity rule for every research compound.

Use validated, compound-specific documentation and stability-indicating testing to determine whether material remains within specification.
