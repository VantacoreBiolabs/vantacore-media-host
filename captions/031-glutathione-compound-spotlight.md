GLUTATHIONE: YES, IT’S A PEPTIDE 🧬

Glutathione is a tripeptide—meaning it is built from three amino acids: glutamate, cysteine and glycine.

Four quick facts:

• Your cells make glutathione through a two-step enzyme pathway.  
• GSH is the reduced form; GSSG is the oxidized form.  
• Cycling between these forms helps cells manage their redox environment.  
• Glutathione works with enzymes such as glutathione peroxidase—it is part of a system, not a stand-alone “detox switch.”

Evidence reality: glutathione’s cellular role is well established, but that does not prove every supplement, injection or wellness claim. Human outcomes depend on the formulation, route, population and question being studied.
