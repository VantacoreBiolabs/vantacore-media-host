WHAT DOES “RESEARCH GRADE” ACTUALLY MEAN? 🔬

It may sound like a quality seal, but the words alone do not prove quality.

Four things to know:

• The phrase generally signals that a material is intended for laboratory research—not approved human use.  
• It does not establish FDA approval, clinical effectiveness or human safety.  
• A purity number does not automatically prove identity, sterility, endotoxin control or correct fill quantity.  
• Meaningful evaluation requires lot-specific documentation, appropriate analytical methods and traceability.

Important limitation: usage of “research grade” can vary between sellers and product categories. Treat it as an intended-use label—not a substitute for evidence.
