GHK-Cu: THE COPPER-BINDING TRIPEPTIDE 🧬

GHK-Cu is easier to understand than its name suggests.

Four quick facts:

• GHK is a three-amino-acid peptide made from glycine, histidine and lysine.  
• It binds copper(II), forming the GHK-Cu complex.  
• Researchers have studied it in skin biology, wound-healing models and tissue remodeling.  
• Most of the attention centers on how it may influence extracellular-matrix signals, including collagen-related processes.

Evidence check: laboratory, animal and topical-skin findings should not be treated as proof for injectable use. FDA notes that human safety data for compounded injectable GHK-Cu are limited and has identified potential immunogenicity concerns related to aggregation and peptide impurities.
