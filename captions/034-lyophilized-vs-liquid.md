LYOPHILIZED vs LIQUID: WHAT CHANGES? ❄️💧

The difference is the material’s physical form—not automatically its identity or quality.

Four quick facts:

• Lyophilization, or freeze-drying, removes water from a frozen material under vacuum.  
• A liquid preparation already has the material dissolved or dispersed in a solution.  
• Removing water can improve stability for some materials, but “freeze-dried” does not mean indestructible.  
• Temperature, moisture, light, packaging and formulation can still affect either form.

Evidence check: storage conditions and usable time cannot be assumed from appearance or physical form. They must be supported by product-specific stability data—including stability after reconstitution when applicable.
