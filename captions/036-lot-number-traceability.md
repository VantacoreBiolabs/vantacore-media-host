LOT NUMBER: THE LINK BETWEEN A SAMPLE AND ITS RECORDS 🔗

A product name tells you what the item is supposed to be. A lot number helps identify the specific batch it came from.

Four things to check:

• The container should carry a readable lot, batch or control identifier.  
• That identifier should match the lot listed on the Certificate of Analysis.  
• Lot-specific records connect test results, manufacturing dates and quality decisions to one batch.  
• Traceability helps investigators identify what may be affected when a deviation, complaint or recall occurs.

Evidence check: a lot number supports traceability, but it does not prove that testing was accurate or that the material met every specification. The records and methods behind it still matter.
