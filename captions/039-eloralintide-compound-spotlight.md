ELORALINTIDE: SELECTIVE AMYLIN SIGNALING 🧬

Eloralintide, also called LY3841136, is an investigational research compound in the amylin-receptor field.

Four quick facts:

• It is designed as a long-acting, selective amylin-receptor agonist.  
• Laboratory studies found greater activity at amylin receptors than at the calcitonin receptor.  
• A randomized, double-blind phase 2 trial studied it for 48 weeks in adults with obesity or overweight without diabetes.  
• A larger phase 3 research program is now evaluating its effectiveness and safety.

Evidence check: phase 2 findings are research signals—not proof of approval, long-term safety or individual results. The ongoing phase 3 studies are designed to provide stronger evidence.
