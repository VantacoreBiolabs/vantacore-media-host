ACCURACY vs PRECISION: KNOW THE DIFFERENCE 🎯

A result can be consistent without being correct.

Four quick facts:

• Accuracy asks how close a measurement is to an accepted reference value.  
• Precision asks how closely repeated measurements agree with one another.  
• Results can be precise but inaccurate if they cluster together away from the correct value.  
• A strong analytical method aims for both—and checks performance across its intended measurement range.

Evidence check: a single good-looking result cannot establish either characteristic. Accuracy and precision are evaluated with suitable reference materials, repeated measurements and a predefined analytical procedure.
