RETATRUTIDE: THREE RECEPTOR PATHWAYS 🧬

Retatrutide is an investigational peptide designed to activate three receptor systems involved in metabolic regulation.

Four quick facts:
• It targets GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors.
• One peptide molecule was engineered to engage all three pathways.
• Phase 2 research tested its effects and safety in adults with obesity or overweight.
• A broader phase 3 program is evaluating effectiveness and safety in larger groups.

Evidence check: retatrutide is not FDA-approved. Early and mid-stage findings—even encouraging ones—do not guarantee individual results or establish the full long-term safety picture.
