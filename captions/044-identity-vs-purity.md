IDENTITY vs PURITY: TWO DIFFERENT QUESTIONS 🔬

A sample can look highly pure and still need a separate identity check.

Four quick facts:
• Identity testing asks whether the expected material is actually present.
• Purity testing estimates how much of the measured sample is the main component versus detected impurities.
• Identity and purity may require different procedures, reference materials and validation evidence.
• A high purity result does not automatically confirm identity, strength, sterility or safety.

Evidence check: every analytical method has a defined purpose and limits. A result only supports the question that the method was designed and validated to answer.
