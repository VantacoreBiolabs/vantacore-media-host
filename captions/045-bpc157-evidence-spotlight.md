BPC-157: WHAT THE EVIDENCE REALLY SAYS 🧬

BPC-157 gets plenty of online attention—but popularity and clinical proof are not the same thing.

Four quick facts:
• BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide made from 15 amino acids.
• Researchers have explored it in laboratory and animal models involving tissues such as tendons and the digestive tract.
• Proposed mechanisms remain under investigation; there is no single clinically established explanation for its effects.
• Reliable human safety and effectiveness data remain limited.

Evidence check: BPC-157 is not FDA-approved. FDA has highlighted limited safety information and concerns involving immune responses, peptide-related impurities and characterization of the active ingredient.
