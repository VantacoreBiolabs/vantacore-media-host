PK vs PD: WHERE IT GOES vs WHAT IT DOES 🔬

These two research terms describe different parts of the same story.

Four quick facts:
• Pharmacokinetics, or PK, tracks a compound’s movement through the body over time.
• PK commonly examines absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion—often shortened to ADME.
• Pharmacodynamics, or PD, examines the biological response associated with a compound and its target.
• PK/PD analysis links exposure over time with the observed response.

Evidence check: neither PK nor PD alone proves that a compound is safe or clinically effective. Results depend on the population, study design, measurement methods and the specific compound being tested.
