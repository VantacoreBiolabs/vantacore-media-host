AOD-9604: WHAT DID THE RESEARCH FIND? 🧬

AOD-9604 is often promoted online for fat loss—but the full research story is more complicated.

Four quick facts:
• AOD-9604 is a 16-amino-acid peptide derived from the C-terminal region of human growth hormone.
• It was designed for research into fat metabolism without reproducing the full activity of growth hormone.
• Early laboratory and animal studies examined lipid metabolism and body-fat effects.
• A larger randomized obesity trial did not show a statistically significant weight-loss benefit over placebo at its main endpoint.

Evidence check: AOD-9604 is not FDA-approved. FDA says human safety information is limited and has identified concerns involving immune responses, peptide-related impurities and characterization of the active ingredient.
