STERILITY vs ENDOTOXIN: TWO DIFFERENT TESTS 🔬

These quality checks answer different safety questions—and one cannot substitute for the other.

Four quick facts:
• Sterility testing looks for viable microorganisms, including bacteria and fungi, under defined test conditions.
• Bacterial endotoxin testing looks for endotoxins associated with Gram-negative bacteria.
• Endotoxins can remain even when the bacteria that produced them are no longer alive.
• A sterility result does not establish endotoxin control, and an endotoxin result does not establish sterility.

Evidence check: finished-product tests are important, but they do not replace validated manufacturing, contamination controls and appropriate quality systems.
