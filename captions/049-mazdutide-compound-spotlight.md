MAZDUTIDE: ONE PEPTIDE, TWO RECEPTORS 🧬

Mazdutide is a long-acting peptide designed to activate two metabolic receptor pathways.

Four quick facts:
• It is an analogue of oxyntomodulin, a naturally occurring gut hormone.
• It activates both the GLP-1 receptor and the glucagon receptor.
• Researchers have studied it in adults with obesity, overweight and type 2 diabetes.
• Randomized phase 2 and phase 3 trials have reported changes in body weight and metabolic measures.

Evidence check: mazdutide is approved in China for specified indications, but it is not FDA-approved in the United States. Most pivotal published research has involved Chinese participants, so regulatory status and evidence should not be generalized across countries or populations.
