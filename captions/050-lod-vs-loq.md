LOD vs LOQ: DETECTED DOESN'T MEAN QUANTIFIED 🔬

These two limits answer different laboratory questions.

Four quick facts:
• The limit of detection, or LOD, is the lowest amount a method can reliably distinguish from background noise.
• The limit of quantitation, or LOQ, is the lowest amount that can be measured with suitable accuracy and precision.
• A signal can be detectable yet still be too low for a dependable numerical result.
• LOD and LOQ depend on the analyte, method, equipment and sample matrix.

Evidence check: these are validated method-performance limits—not universal numbers. “Not detected” usually means below that method’s detection capability; it does not prove the amount is absolutely zero.
