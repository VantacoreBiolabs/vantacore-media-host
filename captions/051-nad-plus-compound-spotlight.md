NAD+: NOT A PEPTIDE—A CELLULAR COENZYME ⚡

NAD+ is involved in hundreds of enzyme-driven reactions throughout the body.

Four quick facts:
• NAD+ stands for nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide.
• It is a coenzyme, not a peptide or hormone.
• NAD+ and NADH help transfer electrons during cellular energy metabolism.
• NAD+ is also used by enzymes involved in cell signaling, gene regulation and DNA repair.

Evidence check: NAD+ has essential roles inside cells, but that does not automatically prove that an NAD+ product improves energy, slows aging or treats disease. Evidence depends on the formulation, route, population and outcome studied.
