EXPIRATION DATE vs RETEST DATE: NOT THE SAME ⏳

Both rely on stability data, but they lead to different next steps.

Four quick facts:
• An expiration date marks the period during which a material is expected to remain within specifications under defined storage conditions.
• After that expiration date, the material should not be used.
• A retest date marks when a material should be re-examined to confirm that it is still suitable for use.
• Passing a scientifically appropriate retest can support continued use when the applicable quality system allows it.

Evidence check: neither date is a guess or a guarantee under every condition. Packaging, temperature, humidity, light exposure and actual storage history all matter, and retesting cannot erase damage from improper handling.
