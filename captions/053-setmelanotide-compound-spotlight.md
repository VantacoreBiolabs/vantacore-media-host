SETMELANOTIDE: A RARE-PATHWAY PEPTIDE 🧬

Setmelanotide is not a general weight-loss compound. It targets a specific brain signaling pathway and is approved only for defined rare conditions.

Four quick facts:
• Setmelanotide is an eight-amino-acid cyclic peptide modeled on alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone.
• It activates the melanocortin-4 receptor, or MC4R—a pathway involved in hunger, fullness and energy regulation.
• Its U.S. approvals apply to specific forms of syndromic, monogenic or acquired hypothalamic obesity.
• The exact eligible condition matters; this approval should not be generalized to common obesity.

Evidence check: FDA approval does not mean a medicine is appropriate for everyone. The current prescribing information includes important limitations, warnings and monitoring requirements.
