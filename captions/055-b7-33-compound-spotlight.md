B7-33: RELAXIN-PATHWAY RESEARCH 🧬

B7-33 is an experimental synthetic peptide designed to reproduce selected signals from human relaxin-2 in a simpler, single-chain structure.

Four quick facts:
• B7-33 was built from part of the B-chain of human relaxin-2.
• It activates relaxin family peptide receptor 1, better known as RXFP1.
• Researchers describe it as functionally selective because it favors some downstream signals more than others.
• Cell and animal studies have explored fibrosis, tissue remodeling and cardiovascular or lung injury models.

Evidence check: the published findings are preclinical. Mouse and laboratory results do not establish human safety or effectiveness, and B7-33 is not an FDA-approved medicine.
