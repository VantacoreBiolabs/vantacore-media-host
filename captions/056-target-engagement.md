TARGET ENGAGEMENT: BINDING IS ONE STEP 🎯

In target-based research, scientists ask whether a compound actually reaches and interacts with the biological target it was designed for.

Four quick facts:
• A target can be a receptor, enzyme, protein or another biological structure.
• Target engagement means the compound physically interacts with that intended target in the system being studied.
• Researchers can measure engagement with laboratory assays, imaging, tracers or target-linked biomarkers.
• Engagement supports a proposed mechanism—but it does not automatically prove a useful biological or clinical outcome.

Evidence check: a compound may bind its target yet still lack adequate exposure, selectivity, downstream effect, safety or clinical effectiveness. Target engagement is one link in the evidence chain, not the finish line.
