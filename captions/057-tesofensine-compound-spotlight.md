TESOFENSINE: NOT A PEPTIDE 🧠

Tesofensine is an investigational synthetic small molecule—not an amino-acid peptide.

Four quick facts:
• It inhibits reuptake of three monoamine messengers: serotonin, dopamine and norepinephrine.
• This can change signaling related to appetite, reward and energy balance.
• It was first researched in neurological conditions, where researchers also noticed changes in body weight.
• A 24-week randomized phase 2 obesity trial reported greater average weight loss than placebo, alongside effects such as dry mouth, nausea, constipation and increased heart rate.

Evidence check: the phase 2 paper later received an expression of concern and criticism concerning adverse-event reporting. Tesofensine remains investigational and is not FDA-approved; one older trial cannot establish its full safety or effectiveness.
