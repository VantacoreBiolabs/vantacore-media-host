LINACLOTIDE: A PEPTIDE THAT ACTS LOCALLY 🧬

Some peptides are designed to work mainly at a local surface instead of circulating widely through the body.

Four quick facts:
• Linaclotide is a 14-amino-acid peptide.
• It activates guanylate cyclase-C, or GC-C, on the intestinal lining.
• That signal raises cyclic GMP and promotes chloride, bicarbonate and fluid movement into the intestine.
• Linaclotide and its active metabolite act locally on the intestinal surface, with minimal systemic absorption described in the prescribing information.

Evidence check: linaclotide has FDA-approved uses for specific constipation-related conditions, but that does not make it a general “gut wellness” peptide. Its label includes important age restrictions, a boxed warning concerning serious dehydration in children younger than two, and diarrhea as a major adverse reaction.
