PRAMLINTIDE: THE ENGINEERED AMYLIN ANALOG 🧬

Pramlintide is a synthetic 37-amino-acid peptide modeled after amylin, a hormone normally released alongside insulin by pancreatic beta cells.

Four quick facts:
• It differs from human amylin at three positions, where proline substitutions help make the peptide more suitable as a medicine.
• It slows gastric emptying, which changes how quickly nutrients reach the intestine.
• It helps suppress post-meal glucagon signaling.
• It was first approved in the United States in 2005 for specific adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes who use mealtime insulin.

Evidence check: pramlintide is an approved prescription medicine—not a general wellness peptide. Its labeling includes a boxed warning for severe hypoglycemia when used with insulin, along with important patient-selection requirements and contraindications.
