ICATIBANT: A PEPTIDE THAT BLOCKS A RECEPTOR 🧬

Icatibant is a synthetic 10-amino-acid peptidomimetic designed to resemble parts of bradykinin while blocking its main receptor.

Four quick facts:
• Bradykinin normally activates the bradykinin B2 receptor.
• That signaling can increase blood-vessel permeability and contribute to swelling.
• Icatibant competes for the B2 receptor and blocks bradykinin signaling there.
• Controlled clinical trials supported its approval for a specific rare-disease use.

Evidence check: icatibant is approved for acute hereditary-angioedema attacks in adults. That does not mean it is established for every type or cause of swelling, and its prescribing information includes limitations, precautions and adverse reactions.
