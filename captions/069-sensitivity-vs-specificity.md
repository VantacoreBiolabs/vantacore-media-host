SENSITIVITY vs SPECIFICITY: FIND OR CLEAR? 🧪

These two numbers describe different parts of a diagnostic test’s performance.

Four quick facts:
• Sensitivity is the percentage of people with the condition who test positive—the true-positive rate.
• Higher sensitivity means fewer affected people are missed as false negatives.
• Specificity is the percentage of people without the condition who test negative—the true-negative rate.
• Higher specificity means fewer unaffected people receive false-positive results.

Evidence check: sensitivity and specificity do not directly tell you the chance that one positive or negative result is correct. That interpretation also depends on factors such as disease prevalence, pretest probability, the testing threshold and the reference standard used to evaluate the test.
