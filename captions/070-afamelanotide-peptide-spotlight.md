AFAMELANOTIDE: THE LIGHT-SENSITIVITY PEPTIDE ☀️

Afamelanotide is a synthetic 13-amino-acid peptide designed as an analog of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone, or α-MSH.

Here’s the quick version:

• It binds predominantly to MC1R, a receptor involved in skin-pigment signaling.
• It increases production of eumelanin—the darker form of melanin—without requiring sunlight or artificial UV exposure.
• Its evidence and approval are narrow: it is approved for adults with erythropoietic protoporphyria, or EPP, to increase pain-free light exposure.
• It is not a general tanning product and does not replace sunscreen or other light-protection measures.

Important limitation: the prescribing information notes that existing moles and freckles may darken and recommends regular full-body skin examinations. Serious hypersensitivity reactions have also been reported.
