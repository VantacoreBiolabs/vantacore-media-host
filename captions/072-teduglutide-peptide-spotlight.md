TEDUGLUTIDE: THE INTESTINAL GLP-2 ANALOG 🧬

Teduglutide is a 33-amino-acid peptide designed as an analog of glucagon-like peptide-2, or GLP-2—a natural signal released by cells in the lower intestine.

Four quick facts:

• It activates GLP-2 receptors found in specific intestinal cell and nerve populations.
• That signaling triggers local mediators involved in intestinal adaptation.
• Clinical research found increased intestinal fluid absorption along with changes in villus height and crypt depth.
• Its approved use is narrow: adults and children at least one year old with short bowel syndrome who depend on parenteral support.

Evidence check: teduglutide is an approved prescription medicine, not a general “gut health” peptide. Its labeling requires monitoring and includes warnings about accelerated abnormal-cell growth, intestinal obstruction, biliary or pancreatic disease, and fluid overload.
