PLECANATIDE: THE INTESTINAL SIGNAL PEPTIDE 🧬

Plecanatide is a 16-amino-acid peptide designed as an analog of uroguanylin, a natural signaling peptide made in the intestine.

Four quick facts:

• It binds guanylate cyclase-C, or GC-C, on the intestinal surface.
• That activation raises cyclic GMP, or cGMP, inside and outside intestinal cells.
• The signal promotes chloride and bicarbonate movement into the intestine, increasing fluid and helping accelerate transit.
• Its approved uses are specific to adults with chronic idiopathic constipation or irritable bowel syndrome with constipation.

Evidence check: plecanatide is an approved prescription medicine—not a general digestive-wellness peptide. Its labeling carries a boxed warning about serious dehydration in pediatric patients, and severe diarrhea can occur.
