VOSORITIDE: THE CNP SIGNALING PEPTIDE 🧬

Vosoritide is a 39-amino-acid peptide analog of C-type natriuretic peptide, or CNP.

Four quick facts:

• Achondroplasia is driven by overactive FGFR3 signaling that limits endochondral bone growth.
• Vosoritide binds natriuretic peptide receptor-B, or NPR-B.
• That signal counteracts part of the downstream FGFR3–MAPK pathway and supports chondrocyte proliferation and differentiation while growth plates remain open.
• Its approved use is specific: increasing linear growth in pediatric patients with achondroplasia and open growth plates.

Evidence check: approval was granted under the accelerated-approval pathway based on improved annualized growth velocity. Continued approval may depend on confirmatory evidence. The label also warns that temporary decreases in blood pressure can occur.
