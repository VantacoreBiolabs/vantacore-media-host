LANREOTIDE: THE SOMATOSTATIN SIGNAL ANALOG 🧬

Lanreotide is a synthetic cyclic peptide modeled after somatostatin—a natural hormone that helps regulate the release of other hormones.

Four quick facts:

• Lanreotide is an eight-amino-acid peptide.
• It binds somatostatin receptors, with its strongest affinity for the SSTR2 subtype.
• That signaling can reduce growth-hormone release and influence several digestive-hormone pathways.
• Its approved uses are specific and include certain settings involving acromegaly, gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumors and carcinoid syndrome.

Evidence check: lanreotide is an approved prescription medicine—not a general hormone-balancing peptide. Its labeling includes monitoring considerations involving the gallbladder, blood glucose, heart rate and thyroid function.
