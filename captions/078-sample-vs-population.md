SAMPLE vs POPULATION: WHO WAS ACTUALLY STUDIED? 🔬

These terms connect a study’s measurements to the larger group researchers want to understand.

Four quick facts:

• The population is the complete group the research question is about.
• The sample is the smaller group actually selected and measured.
• Researchers use sample results to estimate characteristics of the population.
• A larger sample can improve precision, but size alone does not make a sample representative.

Evidence check: selection method matters. If some types of people are systematically missed, a large sample can still produce biased estimates. Sampling uncertainty is also different from errors caused by nonresponse, inaccurate answers, coverage problems or data processing.
