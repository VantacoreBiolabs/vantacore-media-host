IN SPEC vs OUT OF SPEC: WHAT DOES IT ACTUALLY MEAN? 🔬

A specification is the rulebook used to judge a test result—not the result itself.

Four quick facts:

• A specification lists required tests, the analytical procedures used and the acceptance criteria for each test.
• A test result is the value produced when a sample is measured with that procedure.
• “In specification” means the result meets the defined limit, range or other acceptance criterion.
• “Out of specification,” or OOS, means the result falls outside those established criteria.

Evidence check: an OOS result should trigger a documented investigation; simply repeating a test until one passes is not a valid explanation. Passing a listed test also does not prove every quality attribute—specifications are only one part of a broader quality-control strategy.
