DESMOPRESSIN: THE WATER-BALANCE PEPTIDE ANALOG 💧

Desmopressin is a synthetic peptide analog of arginine vasopressin—the natural antidiuretic hormone often shortened to ADH.

Four quick facts:

• It primarily activates vasopressin V2 receptors.
• In the kidneys, those receptors help increase water reabsorption.
• The result is more concentrated urine and less urine production.
• Approved uses vary by formulation and include specific settings such as central diabetes insipidus, nocturia caused by nighttime overproduction of urine, and certain bleeding disorders.

Evidence check: desmopressin is an approved prescription medicine—not a general hydration aid. Because it makes the body retain water, it can cause dangerously low blood sodium, called hyponatremia. Some formulations carry a boxed warning and require formulation-specific screening and monitoring.
