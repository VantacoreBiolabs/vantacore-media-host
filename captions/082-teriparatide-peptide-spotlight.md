TERIPARATIDE: THE BONE-FORMATION PEPTIDE ANALOG 🦴

Teriparatide is a 34-amino-acid peptide matching the biologically active end of human parathyroid hormone, or PTH.

Four quick facts:

• It is also called PTH(1–34) because it contains the first 34 amino acids of the natural 84-amino-acid hormone.
• It activates PTH1 receptors involved in calcium, phosphate and bone metabolism.
• Its signaling preferentially stimulates osteoblast activity and promotes new bone formation.
• Its approved uses are limited to specific adults with osteoporosis who have a high fracture risk or cannot use other available therapies.

Evidence check: teriparatide is an approved prescription medicine—not a general bone-building peptide. Labeling advises against use in people with increased baseline risk of osteosarcoma and includes precautions involving high calcium, kidney stones and temporary drops in blood pressure.
