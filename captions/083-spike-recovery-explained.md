Spike recovery asks a simple question: did the method find what was deliberately added?

• A known amount of the target analyte is added to a sample.
• The sample then goes through the full preparation and measurement process.
• The result is compared with the amount expected after that addition.
• Consistent recovery can show that the workflow handles the analyte reliably; recovery does not always need to be exactly 100%.

Important limitation: recovery is only one piece of method validation. It does not, by itself, establish selectivity, precision, accuracy across the full range, or freedom from matrix effects.
