HAPPENED AFTER ≠ CAUSED BY 🔎

“Adverse event” and “adverse reaction” are not interchangeable.

Four quick facts:

• An adverse event is an unwanted medical occurrence after exposure to a product.
• It can be a symptom, disease, sign or abnormal laboratory result.
• Calling something an adverse event does not require evidence that the product caused it.
• An adverse reaction means a harmful, unintended response for which a causal connection is at least reasonably possible.

Evidence check: one safety report cannot prove causation or tell us how frequently an event occurs. Reviewers examine timing, alternative explanations, repeat patterns and the wider body of evidence before drawing conclusions.
