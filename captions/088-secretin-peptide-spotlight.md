SECRETIN: THE PANCREATIC SIGNAL PEPTIDE 🧬

Secretin is a natural 27-amino-acid hormone—and its laboratory-made human form can help clinicians examine pancreatic function.

• Its amino-acid sequence matches naturally occurring human secretin.
• Its primary action is signaling pancreatic duct cells.
• Those cells release pancreatic fluid containing bicarbonate.
• The response can support specific diagnostic tests of pancreatic function and gastrin secretion.

Evidence check: human secretin is an approved diagnostic agent, not a general digestion or wellness peptide. Test conditions, medications, collection technique and established reference ranges can affect interpretation.
