SYSTEM SUITABILITY: CHECK THE METHOD BEFORE THE SAMPLE 🔬

Before an analytical run begins, the laboratory needs evidence that the measurement system is performing as expected.

• A suitability check uses predefined materials and test conditions.
• The results are compared with predetermined acceptance criteria.
• Those criteria may examine features such as repeatability, resolution or peak behavior.
• Testing proceeds only when the system meets the defined requirements.

Evidence check: passing system suitability shows that the method and equipment were ready for that run. It does not prove the identity, purity, potency or overall quality of an unknown sample.
