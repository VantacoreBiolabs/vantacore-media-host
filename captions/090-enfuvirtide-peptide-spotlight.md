ENFUVIRTIDE: THE HIV FUSION-BLOCKING PEPTIDE 🧬

Enfuvirtide is a synthetic 36-amino-acid peptide designed to interrupt one of the steps HIV-1 uses to enter a CD4 immune cell.

• It targets gp41, a protein on the HIV-1 envelope.
• gp41 normally helps bring the viral and cell membranes together.
• Enfuvirtide disrupts the shape changes needed for those membranes to fuse.
• Blocking fusion helps prevent the virus from entering that cell.

Evidence check: enfuvirtide is an approved prescription medicine used with other antiretroviral medicines in specific treatment-experienced patients. It is not a cure, vaccine or stand-alone prevention method, and resistance can reduce its activity.
