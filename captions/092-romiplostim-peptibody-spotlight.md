ROMIPLOSTIM: THE PLATELET-SIGNALING PEPTIBODY 🩸

Romiplostim is not a conventional peptide or an antibody. It is an engineered Fc–peptide fusion protein called a peptibody.

• Its peptide regions bind and activate the thrombopoietin, or TPO, receptor.
• That signal supports platelet production in the bone marrow.
• Its binding peptides do not share the amino-acid sequence of natural TPO.
• Approved uses are limited to specific settings involving immune thrombocytopenia and hematopoietic radiation injury.

Evidence check: romiplostim is an approved prescription medicine—not a general platelet booster. It is not indicated for every cause of a low platelet count, and excessive platelet increases can raise the risk of blood clots.
