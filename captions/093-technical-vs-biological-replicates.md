Not all repeats answer the same question.

Technical replicates are repeated measurements of the same sample. They help reveal variation introduced by the assay, instrument, or handling.

Biological replicates are independent samples tested under the same study question. They help capture real biological variation across experimental units.

The key distinction: measuring one sample three times does not create three independent biological samples. Clear reporting should state what “n” represents and which kind of replicate was used.
