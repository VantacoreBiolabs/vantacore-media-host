LEUPROLIDE: SIGNAL FIRST. QUIET LATER. 🧬

Leuprolide is a synthetic peptide that activates the gonadotropin-releasing hormone, or GnRH, receptor.

• The first signal can temporarily raise the pituitary hormones LH and FSH.
• With continuous signaling, GnRH receptors become less responsive and decrease in number.
• LH and FSH then fall, reducing downstream production of gonadal hormones.
• That is why a receptor agonist can ultimately create a suppressive effect.

Evidence check: leuprolide is an approved prescription medicine with several formulation-specific indications. The initial hormone rise, later suppression, safety considerations and approved use depend on the exact product and clinical setting—it is not a general-purpose “hormone reset.”
