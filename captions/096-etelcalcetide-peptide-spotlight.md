ETELCALCETIDE: AMPLIFYING THE CALCIUM SIGNAL 🧬

Etelcalcetide is a synthetic peptide calcimimetic—it helps a receptor respond more strongly to calcium already outside the cell.

• Its target is the calcium-sensing receptor, or CaSR.
• It enhances calcium-driven activation of that receptor.
• On parathyroid chief cells, CaSR activation decreases parathyroid hormone, or PTH, secretion.
• Its approved use is limited to secondary hyperparathyroidism in adults with chronic kidney disease receiving hemodialysis.

Evidence check: etelcalcetide is a prescription medicine, not a general PTH or calcium supplement. It can lower blood calcium—sometimes severely—so the labeled setting includes clinical monitoring and important safety precautions.
