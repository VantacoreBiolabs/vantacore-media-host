BREMELANOTIDE: A CYCLIC PEPTIDE WITH A CNS TARGET 🧠

Bremelanotide is a synthetic cyclic heptapeptide—a ring-shaped peptide built from seven amino acids.

• It is a melanocortin-receptor agonist.
• It activates several melanocortin receptor subtypes rather than only one.
• At clinically relevant exposure, MC1R and MC4R binding are considered most relevant.
• MC4R-expressing neurons are found in many areas of the central nervous system.

Evidence check: the exact mechanism behind its approved clinical effect remains unknown. Bremelanotide is approved only for a specific population with acquired, generalized HSDD—not for men, postmenopausal women or enhancement of sexual performance. Important label limitations include temporary blood-pressure increases, nausea and possible focal skin darkening.
