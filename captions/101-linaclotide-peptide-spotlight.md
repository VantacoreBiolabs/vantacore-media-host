LINACLOTIDE: A PEPTIDE THAT SIGNALS LOCALLY IN THE GUT 🧬

Linaclotide is a 14-amino-acid peptide designed to activate guanylate cyclase-C, or GC-C, on the intestinal surface.

• It is structurally related to the natural peptides guanylin and uroguanylin.
• It and its active metabolite act locally on the luminal surface of the intestinal epithelium.
• GC-C activation raises cyclic GMP, or cGMP.
• Intracellular cGMP promotes chloride and bicarbonate secretion, which draws fluid into the intestine.

Evidence check: linaclotide is an approved prescription medicine for specific constipation-related conditions—not a general digestive-health peptide. Diarrhea can be severe, and it is contraindicated in children younger than 2 years because of the risk of serious dehydration.
