CARBETOCIN: AN OXYTOCIN ANALOG BUILT TO LAST LONGER

• Carbetocin is a synthetic peptide analog of oxytocin.
• It binds oxytocin receptors in uterine smooth muscle.
• That receptor signal promotes rhythmic uterine contraction.
• Its action lasts longer than oxytocin’s.

Evidence boundary: Carbetocin is a prescription obstetric medicine. Evidence, approval, and clinical use are context-specific—not a general wellness claim.
