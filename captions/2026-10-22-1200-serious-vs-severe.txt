SERIOUS ≠ SEVERE: THEY ANSWER DIFFERENT SAFETY QUESTIONS

• Severe describes intensity: mild, moderate, or severe.
• Serious describes the outcome—not simply how strong symptoms felt.
• Serious outcomes include death, immediate risk of death, hospitalization, significant disability, birth defect, or another important medical event.
• An event can be severe without meeting serious criteria—or serious without being rated severe.

Evidence boundary: Neither label alone proves that a product caused the event. Causality is assessed separately.
