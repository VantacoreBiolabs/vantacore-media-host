BIVALIRUDIN: A 20-AMINO-ACID PEPTIDE THAT DIRECTLY BLOCKS THROMBIN

• Bivalirudin is a synthetic 20-amino-acid peptide analog of hirudin.
• It binds two sites on thrombin: the catalytic site and an anion-binding exosite.
• It inhibits both circulating and clot-bound thrombin.
• Its binding is reversible because thrombin slowly cleaves the peptide.

Pathway: bivalirudin → thrombin blocked → less fibrin formation.

Evidence boundary: Bivalirudin is a prescription anticoagulant for specific clinical uses. Bleeding is its major safety risk.
