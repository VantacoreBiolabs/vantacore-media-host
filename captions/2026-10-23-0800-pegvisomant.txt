PEGVISOMANT: A growth-hormone look-alike built to block the signal.

Pegvisomant is an altered analog of human growth hormone. Instead of switching the growth-hormone receptor on, it binds the receptor and blocks natural growth hormone from activating it. That reduces production of IGF-1, a key downstream signal.

Evidence check: Pegvisomant is an approved prescription medicine for specific adults with acromegaly. Its labeling calls for IGF-1 and liver-test monitoring, and the approved use should not be generalized to other goals.
