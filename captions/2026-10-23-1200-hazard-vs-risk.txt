HAZARD ≠ RISK

A hazard is something with the potential to cause harm. Risk asks a different question: how likely is that harm, and how serious could it be under real exposure conditions?

• Hazard describes the source or situation.
• Exposure helps determine whether contact can occur.
• Risk considers likelihood and possible severity.
• Reducing exposure can reduce risk even when the hazard remains.

Evidence check: Risk estimates depend on the setting, exposure pathway and available evidence. A hazard label alone does not quantify real-world risk.
