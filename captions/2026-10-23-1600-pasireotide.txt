PASIREOTIDE: One cyclic peptide, four receptor targets.

Pasireotide is a six-amino-acid cyclic peptide and a laboratory-designed analog of somatostatin.

• It binds somatostatin receptors.
• It has high affinity for SSTR1, SSTR2, SSTR3 and SSTR5.
• In Cushing’s disease, receptor activation can suppress ACTH signaling.
• Lower ACTH signaling can reduce downstream cortisol production.

Evidence check: Pasireotide is a prescription medicine for specific endocrine uses. Hyperglycemia is an important risk, and official labeling calls for glucose monitoring. This mechanism overview is not treatment guidance.
