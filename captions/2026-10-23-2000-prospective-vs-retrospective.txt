PROSPECTIVE VS. RETROSPECTIVE: Which way does the study look?

In a prospective design, researchers define participants first and observe outcomes moving forward. In a retrospective design, researchers analyze records or events that already exist.

• Prospective studies follow forward from a defined starting point.
• Retrospective studies look back through existing data.
• Both designs can answer useful research questions.
• Both can be affected by bias and incomplete data.

Evidence check: These labels describe timing and data collection—not automatic quality. The specific study design, measurements, missing data and bias still matter.
