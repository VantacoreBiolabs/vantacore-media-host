SINCALIDE: The eight-amino-acid signal that makes the gallbladder move.

Sincalide is a synthetic version of the active eight-amino-acid end of cholecystokinin, often called CCK-8.

• It triggers gallbladder contraction.
• It moves bile in a way similar to natural CCK signaling.
• It stimulates pancreatic secretion.
• It increases intestinal movement and can slow stomach emptying.

Evidence check: Sincalide is a prescription diagnostic agent, not a general wellness compound. Serious hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis, can occur.
