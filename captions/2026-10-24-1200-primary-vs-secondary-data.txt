PRIMARY DATA ≠ SECONDARY DATA

New collection or existing records?

Primary data are collected first-hand for the current study—think surveys, interviews and observations.

Secondary data already exist and are reused for a new question—think registries, records and published datasets.

Primary data can offer more control over what is measured. Secondary data can save time and widen scale.

Evidence check: neither type is automatically better. Fit, quality, bias and definitions still matter.
