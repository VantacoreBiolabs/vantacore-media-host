ECALLANTIDE: THE 60-AMINO-ACID KALLIKREIN BLOCKER

Ecallantide is a recombinant 60-amino-acid protein.

It selectively and reversibly inhibits plasma kallikrein—an enzyme that helps generate bradykinin.

Less kallikrein activity means less bradykinin production. That pathway matters because excess bradykinin contributes to the swelling seen during hereditary angioedema attacks.

Ecallantide is FDA-approved for acute hereditary angioedema attacks in people ages 12 and older.

Evidence check: the prescribing information carries a boxed warning because anaphylaxis can occur. It requires administration and monitoring by a healthcare professional with appropriate medical support.
