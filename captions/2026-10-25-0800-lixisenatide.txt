LIXISENATIDE: THE 44-AMINO-ACID GLP-1 SIGNAL

Lixisenatide is a synthetic 44-amino-acid analog of human GLP-1.

It activates the GLP-1 receptor and:
• increases glucose-dependent insulin release
• decreases glucagon secretion
• slows gastric emptying

It is a prescription therapy approved to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes alongside diet and exercise.

Evidence check: it is not for type 1 diabetes. Pancreatitis and serious hypersensitivity are important warnings, and it is not recommended for people with gastroparesis.
