TIRZEPATIDE: ONE PEPTIDE. TWO RECEPTOR SIGNALS.

Here’s the molecular design in plain English:

• Tirzepatide is a synthetic peptide built from 39 amino acids.
• Its sequence is based on the natural GIP hormone signal.
• It activates both GIP and GLP-1 receptors.
• A C20 fatty diacid helps it bind albumin, which extends how long the molecule remains in circulation.

Evidence limit: molecular structure explains receptor activity and duration, but it does not predict an individual person’s outcomes or experience.
