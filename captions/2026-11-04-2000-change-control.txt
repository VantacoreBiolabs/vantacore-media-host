CHANGE CONTROL: SMALL CHANGE. BIG QUESTION.

In quality systems, even a “small” change deserves a structured review.

• Describe exactly what will change and why.
• Assess possible effects on the process, data, and product quality.
• Use science and risk to decide whether the change should be approved.
• After implementation, verify that the goal was met without unintended consequences.

Evidence limit: documentation and review help manage uncertainty; they do not automatically prove that a change is harmless.
