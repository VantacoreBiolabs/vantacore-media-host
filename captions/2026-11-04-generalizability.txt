CAN THESE RESULTS TRAVEL?

A study can be well designed and still apply only to a narrow group.

• Generalizability asks whether findings extend beyond the people and setting studied.
• Eligibility rules shape who gets represented.
• Age, health, location, timing, and how an intervention is delivered can change relevance.
• A diverse sample helps—but matching the target population matters most.

Evidence limit: no study automatically applies to everyone. Check who was studied, where, when, and under what conditions.
