CARPERITIDE: THE HEART-SIGNAL COPY

This peptide recreates a signal naturally released by the heart.

• Carperitide is a recombinant form of human atrial natriuretic peptide, or ANP.
• It contains 28 amino acids.
• A disulfide bond forms the ring structure shared by mature ANP.
• It activates ANP receptor-A, a membrane guanylyl cyclase that raises the second messenger cGMP.

Evidence limit: approved use varies by country—carperitide is available in Japan but is not FDA-approved in the United States. Knowing the pathway does not predict every outcome.
