AUDIT TRAIL: WHO CHANGED WHAT?

An audit trail is a secure, computer-generated, time-stamped record.

It can show who created, changed, or deleted an electronic record.

It preserves when an action happened and, when required, why it happened.

That history helps reviewers reconstruct the sequence of events around the data.

Evidence limit: An audit trail supports traceability, but its existence alone does not prove that data are complete, accurate, or trustworthy.
