GOSERELIN: THE SIGNAL THAT TURNS DOWN ITS OWN PATHWAY

Goserelin is a synthetic 10-amino-acid analog of GnRH, a natural signaling peptide.

It binds GnRH receptors in the pituitary—the same pathway used by the native signal.

Early exposure stimulates signaling and can raise pituitary gonadotropin output.

With sustained exposure, the receptor pathway becomes desensitized and gonadotropin secretion is suppressed.

Evidence limit: This simplified pathway explains the core mechanism, but it does not predict every individual response or establish safety for any particular person.
