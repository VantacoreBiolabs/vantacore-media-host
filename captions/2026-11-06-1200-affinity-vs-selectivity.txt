AFFINITY ≠ SELECTIVITY

Affinity asks: How tightly does a molecule bind to one target?

Selectivity asks: How strongly does it prefer that target over other targets?

A compound can bind one target tightly and still bind several other targets too.

Selectivity is relative—not absolute—and it can decrease as concentration rises.

Evidence limit: Binding measurements describe target interactions, but they do not by themselves prove a whole-cell effect or predict what happens in a person.
