CHAIN OF CUSTODY: CAN YOU TRACE THE SAMPLE?

A chain-of-custody record follows a sample from collection through transfer, storage, analysis, and final disposition.

It records who handled the sample and when each handoff occurred.

It can document storage conditions and whether custody seals remained intact.

That history helps reviewers spot missing handoffs, mix-ups, or possible tampering concerns.

Evidence limit: A complete custody log supports traceability, but it does not by itself prove sample identity or result accuracy.
