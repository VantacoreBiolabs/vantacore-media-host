Difelikefalin: a peptide designed to keep its signal mainly outside the brain.

• It is a synthetic tetrapeptide built from D-amino acids.
• It activates kappa opioid receptors (KORs).
• It is soluble in water.
• Its chemistry limits passive penetration into the brain.

Evidence limit: The relevance of KOR activation to its observed effects is not fully established, and “peripherally restricted” does not mean zero central exposure.
