Aliquots: one sample, many portions.

• An aliquot is a measured part taken from a larger sample.
• Each portion is stored in its own labeled container.
• Separate portions can reduce repeated freeze–thaw of the original sample.
• Every aliquot should stay linked to its source and handling history.

Evidence limit: Aliquoting assumes the starting material is uniform. If it is not mixed or representative, the smaller portions may differ.
