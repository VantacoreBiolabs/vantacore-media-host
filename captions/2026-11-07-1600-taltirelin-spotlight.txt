Taltirelin: a modified signal modeled after TRH.

• It is a synthetic oligopeptide joined by peptide bonds.
• Its structure is based on thyrotropin-releasing hormone, or TRH.
• It activates the human TRH receptor.
• Preclinical studies found greater stability than natural TRH.

Evidence limit: Much of the mechanistic and stability evidence comes from animal or model-cell studies, so findings depend on the experimental system.
