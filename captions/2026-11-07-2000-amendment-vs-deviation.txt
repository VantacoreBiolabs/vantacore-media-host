Protocol amendment vs protocol deviation: planned change or departure from the plan?

• An amendment formally changes or clarifies the protocol.
• Significant amendments are usually reviewed before implementation.
• A deviation is a departure from the approved protocol.
• Both should be documented and assessed for their impact.

Evidence limit: Exact reporting rules vary by study and regulator. Changes needed to reduce an immediate hazard may be handled differently under defined requirements.
