ACTH 1–39: the full-length peptide signal behind the adrenal pathway.

• It is a 39-amino-acid peptide produced by pituitary corticotroph cells.
• It is processed from the larger POMC precursor protein.
• It binds the melanocortin 2 receptor, or MC2R.
• MC2R activation starts a cAMP-based signaling cascade.

Evidence limit: ACTH 1–39 and the shorter ACTH 1–24 fragment share an active region, but they are related molecules—not identical forms.
