A LAB RESULT HAS 3 RISK WINDOWS.

Before the test (pre-analytical): collection, labeling, transport, and storage.

During the test (analytical): sample preparation, measurement, and data review.

After the test (post-analytical): verification, reporting, interpretation, and follow-up.

Quality means checking the full process—not only the instrument.

Evidence/limitation: These phase labels help organize risk, but they do not identify a root cause by themselves. Exact workflows and responsibilities vary by test and laboratory.
