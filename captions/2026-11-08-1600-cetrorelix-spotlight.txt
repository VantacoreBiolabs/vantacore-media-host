CETRORELIX: A GnRH RECEPTOR ANTAGONIST.

• It is a synthetic decapeptide—ten amino-acid positions linked in one chain.

• It is an analog of natural gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH), with substitutions at five positions.

• It competes with natural GnRH for receptors on pituitary cells.

• As an antagonist, it reduces downstream LH and FSH release without first switching the receptor on.

Evidence/limitation: DailyMed and IUPHAR describe this peptide-receptor mechanism. A known mechanism does not, by itself, predict every response or settle all safety questions.
