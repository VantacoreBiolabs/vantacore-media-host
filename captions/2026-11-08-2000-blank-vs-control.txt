BLANK vs KNOWN CONTROL: NOT THE SAME CHECK.

BLANK
• Designed without the target being measured.
• Looks for unwanted background signal or contamination.

KNOWN CONTROL
• Contains a verified amount or expected result.
• Checks whether the measurement process performs as expected.

Evidence/limitation: A blank is itself one type of control. Exact blank types, control materials, and acceptance rules depend on the method.
