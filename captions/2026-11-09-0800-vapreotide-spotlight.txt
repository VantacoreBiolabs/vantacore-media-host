VAPREOTIDE: THE 8-POSITION SIGNAL RING.

• Vapreotide is a synthetic cyclic octapeptide—eight linked amino-acid positions.

• It is an analog of the natural signaling peptide somatostatin.

• A disulfide bond between two cysteine residues helps close its peptide ring.

• IUPHAR reports agonist activity mainly at human SSTR2 and SSTR5, with activity also reported at SSTR3.

Evidence/limitation: Receptor activity can vary by subtype, species, and assay. A receptor profile alone does not establish benefit or safety in every context.
