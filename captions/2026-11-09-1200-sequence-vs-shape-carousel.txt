SEQUENCE ≠ SHAPE.

• Sequence is the specific order of amino acids in a peptide chain.

• Even the same amino-acid types can form a different molecule when their order changes.

• Folding, cyclic bonds, and disulfide bridges help create three-dimensional structure.

• Shape influences how a peptide interacts with receptors and other molecules.

Evidence/limitation: Sequence strongly guides structure, but environment, chemical modifications, and experimental conditions also matter. Sequence alone does not fully predict behavior.
