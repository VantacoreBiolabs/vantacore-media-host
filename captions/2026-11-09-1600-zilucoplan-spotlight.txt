ZILUCOPLAN: 15 AMINO ACIDS. ONE COMPLEMENT TARGET.

• Zilucoplan is a synthetic macrocyclic peptide made from 15 amino acids.

• It binds to complement protein C5.

• That binding blocks C5 from being split into C5a and C5b.

• This limits formation of the downstream C5b-9 terminal complement complex.

Evidence/limitation: The mechanism is supported by current regulatory labeling and laboratory studies, but knowing a molecular target does not predict every clinical outcome or safety consideration.
