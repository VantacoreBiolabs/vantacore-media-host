DULAGLUTIDE: NOT JUST ONE PEPTIDE CHAIN.

• Dulaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist built as a fusion protein.

• It contains two identical chains joined by disulfide bonds.

• Each chain carries a modified GLP-1 analog sequence.

• A small peptide linker connects that GLP-1 analog to a modified human IgG4 Fc region.

Evidence/limitation: Current FDA labeling clearly describes this structure and GLP-1-receptor activity, but molecular design alone does not predict every individual outcome or safety consideration.
