THYMOPENTIN: FIVE AMINO ACIDS, ONE ACTIVE FRAGMENT.

• Thymopentin is a synthetic pentapeptide—meaning it contains five amino acids.

• Its sequence is Arg-Lys-Asp-Val-Tyr, shortened to RKDVY.

• It matches amino-acid positions 32–36 of the larger thymopoietin molecule.

• Early laboratory studies examined its effects on T-cell differentiation and intracellular cGMP signaling.

Evidence/limitation: Thymopentin’s sequence and origin are well documented, but much of the foundational biological evidence is older and preclinical. Structure and laboratory activity do not establish broad clinical benefit or safety.
