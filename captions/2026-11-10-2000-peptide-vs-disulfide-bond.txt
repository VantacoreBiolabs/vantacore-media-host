PEPTIDE BOND vs DISULFIDE BOND: ONE BUILDS THE CHAIN. ONE HELPS LOCK THE SHAPE.

• A peptide bond connects the carboxyl group of one amino acid to the amino group of the next.

• Repeating peptide bonds create the chain’s continuous backbone.

• A disulfide bond forms between the sulfur atoms of two cysteine side chains.

• That covalent bridge can link two regions of one chain or connect two separate chains.

Evidence/limitation: Not every peptide contains a disulfide bond. These bonds can strongly influence structure, but sequence, environment, and other molecular interactions also help determine the final three-dimensional shape.
