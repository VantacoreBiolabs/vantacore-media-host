SPEXIN: 14 AMINO ACIDS, TWO GALANIN RECEPTORS.

• Spexin is a signaling peptide made from 14 amino acids.

• Its conserved sequence is Asn-Trp-Thr-Pro-Gln-Ala-Met-Leu-Tyr-Leu-Lys-Gly-Ala-Gln, often written NWTPQAMLYLKGAQ-NH₂.

• Cells make it from a larger precursor encoded by the SPX gene.

• Laboratory studies identify spexin as an agonist at galanin receptors GALR2 and GALR3.

Evidence/limitation: Spexin’s sequence and receptor activity are well documented, but most functional evidence is preclinical. Receptor signaling does not establish broad human benefit or safety.
