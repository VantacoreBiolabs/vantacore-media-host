EC₅₀: THE MIDPOINT OF A RESPONSE CURVE.

• EC₅₀ means the concentration that produces a response halfway between an assay’s baseline and that compound’s maximum response.

• It is a concentration—not an administered dose.

• A lower EC₅₀ usually indicates greater apparent potency, but only when results come from comparable assay conditions.

• EC₅₀ describes potency, not efficacy. The maximum response is described separately by Emax.

Evidence/limitation: EC₅₀ depends on the biological system, receptor expression, concentration range, controls, and curve-fitting method. It is not a universal constant or a prediction of human outcomes.
