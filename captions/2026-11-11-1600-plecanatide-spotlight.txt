PLECANATIDE: 16 AMINO ACIDS, TWO DISULFIDE BRIDGES.

• Plecanatide is a synthetic 16-amino-acid peptide.

• Its sequence is Asn-Asp-Glu-Cys-Glu-Leu-Cys-Val-Asn-Val-Ala-Cys-Thr-Gly-Cys-Leu, abbreviated NDECELCVNVACTGCL.

• Two disulfide bridges connect Cys4 to Cys12 and Cys7 to Cys15, helping constrain the peptide’s shape.

• It is a structural analog of human uroguanylin and acts as an agonist at the guanylate cyclase-C receptor, also called GC-C.

Evidence/limitation: Plecanatide’s sequence, disulfide pattern, and receptor target are well documented. A receptor mechanism alone does not describe every biological outcome or safety consideration.
