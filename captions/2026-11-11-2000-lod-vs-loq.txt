LOD vs LOQ: DETECT IT vs MEASURE IT.

• LOD—the limit of detection—is the lowest level where an analytical signal can be reliably distinguished from background noise.

• LOQ—the limit of quantitation—is the lowest level that can be measured with suitable accuracy and precision.

• LOD is usually lower than LOQ. A method may detect that something is present before it can report the amount reliably.

• Both limits belong to a specific analytical procedure, not to the analyte alone.

Evidence/limitation: LOD and LOQ can change with the method, instrument, calibration model, sample matrix, and validation approach. “Not detected” does not necessarily mean “not present.”
