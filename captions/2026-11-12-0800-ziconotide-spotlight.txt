ZICONOTIDE: 25 AMINO ACIDS, THREE DISULFIDE BRIDGES.

• Ziconotide is a synthetic equivalent of omega-conotoxin MVIIA, a peptide first identified from the cone snail Conus magus.
• Its 25-residue sequence is CKGKGAKCSRLMYDCCTGSCRSGKC.
• Three disulfide bridges connect residues 1–16, 8–20, and 15–25.
• It blocks the N-type voltage-sensitive calcium channel Caᵥ2.2.

Evidence check: the sequence, bridge pattern, and channel target are well established. A known mechanism does not, by itself, predict every experimental or human outcome.
