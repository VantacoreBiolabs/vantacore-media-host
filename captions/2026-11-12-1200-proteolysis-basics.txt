PROTEOLYSIS: WHEN ENZYMES CUT THE PEPTIDE CHAIN.

• Proteases break peptide bonds through hydrolysis.
• Endopeptidases cut bonds inside a peptide or protein chain.
• Exopeptidases remove residues from the chain’s ends.
• Cleavage can help turn a larger precursor into a mature peptide—or break a peptide into smaller fragments.

Evidence check: cleavage sites depend on the sequence, three-dimensional structure, enzyme, and experimental conditions. A cut observed in one system may not occur the same way in another.
