PRAMLINTIDE: 37 AMINO ACIDS, THREE PROLINE SWAPS.

• Pramlintide is a synthetic analog of the 37-amino-acid peptide human amylin.
• It replaces the native residues at positions 25, 28, and 29 with proline.
• One disulfide bridge connects Cys2 to Cys7, forming a small loop near the N-terminus.
• Its C-terminus is amidated, and it acts as an agonist at human amylin receptor complexes.

Evidence check: the sequence, substitutions, bridge, amidation, and receptor activity are documented. Receptor pharmacology alone does not predict every experimental or human outcome.
