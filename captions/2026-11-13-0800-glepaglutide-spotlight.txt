GLEPAGLUTIDE: 39 AMINO ACIDS, WITH A SIX-LYSINE TAIL.

• Glepaglutide is a synthetic analog of the 33-amino-acid hormone GLP-2.
• Its full sequence contains 39 natural L-amino acids.
• The final six residues are lysines, forming a distinctive C-terminal tail.
• It acts as an agonist at the GLP-2 receptor.

Evidence check: The sequence and receptor target are documented, but structure and receptor activity alone do not predict every experimental or human outcome.
