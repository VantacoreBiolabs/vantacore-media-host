HYDROPHOBIC VS HYDROPHILIC: HOW SIDE CHAINS MEET WATER.

• Every amino acid has the same basic backbone, but its side chain gives it different chemistry.
• Hydrophobic side chains do not interact favorably with water, so they often cluster away from an aqueous environment.
• Polar or charged hydrophilic side chains can interact favorably with water and often face outward.
• This pattern can influence folding, solubility, and how a peptide interacts with other molecules.

Evidence check: these categories are useful simplifications, not hard boxes. Some side chains have mixed properties, and behavior depends on the whole sequence and environment.
