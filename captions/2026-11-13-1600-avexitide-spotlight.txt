AVEXITIDE: 31 AMINO ACIDS, A GLP-1 SIGNAL BLOCKER.

• Avexitide is an amidated analog of exendin-(9–39), a shortened form of exendin-4.
• Its sequence contains 31 amino acids.
• Unlike GLP-1 receptor agonists, avexitide acts as a GLP-1 receptor antagonist.
• An antagonist occupies the receptor without triggering the same activating signal.

Evidence check: the sequence, amidation, and receptor target are documented. Avexitide remains investigational, and its mechanism alone does not establish safety or effectiveness for any use.
