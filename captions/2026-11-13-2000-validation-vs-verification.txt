METHOD VALIDATION VS METHOD VERIFICATION: PROVE IT FITS VS CONFIRM IT WORKS HERE.

• Validation builds objective evidence that a method is fit for its intended purpose.
• Verification confirms that an established method performs as expected in a specific laboratory.
• Both rely on predefined acceptance criteria, documented data, and suitable reference materials.
• Meaningful changes to a validated method may require additional or full revalidation.

Evidence check: terminology and required studies vary by field, governing standard, intended use, and the extent of any method changes.
