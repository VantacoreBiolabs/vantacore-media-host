HISTRELIN: 9 AMINO ACIDS, A GnRH SIGNAL THAT CHANGES WITH TIME.

• Histrelin is a synthetic nine-amino-acid analog of natural GnRH.
• Its structure includes a modified D-histidine and an N-ethylamide end.
• It acts as a GnRH receptor agonist.
• With continuous exposure, an initial stimulatory phase is followed by receptor down-regulation and desensitization, reducing LH and FSH signaling.

Evidence check: the sequence and time-dependent receptor response are documented in current FDA labeling. Structure and mechanism alone do not predict every outcome, and this post is not a protocol or direction for use.
