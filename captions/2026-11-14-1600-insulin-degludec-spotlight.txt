INSULIN DEGLUDEC: TWO CHAINS, THREE BRIDGES, ONE FATTY-ACID TAIL.

• Insulin degludec is a 50-amino-acid analog of human insulin: a 21-residue A chain plus a 29-residue B chain.
• Three disulfide bonds help hold its two-chain structure together.
• Compared with human insulin, the threonine at B30 is removed.
• A glutamic-acid spacer connects a C16 fatty diacid to lysine at B29.

Evidence check: these structural changes help explain multihexamer formation and albumin binding. Structure alone does not predict an individual response, and this is education—not a protocol or direction for use.
