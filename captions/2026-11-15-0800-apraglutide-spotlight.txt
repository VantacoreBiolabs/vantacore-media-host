APRAGLUTIDE: 33 AMINO ACIDS. FOUR KEY CHANGES.

• Apraglutide is a synthetic 33-amino-acid analog of glucagon-like peptide-2 (GLP-2).
• Compared with native human GLP-2, four positions are changed: 2, 10, 11, and 16.
• It selectively activates the GLP-2 receptor, a pathway studied for intestinal adaptation and absorption.
• Its sequence changes contribute to slower breakdown, high protein binding, and longer persistence in research studies.

Evidence check: the STARS Phase 3 trial reported reduced parenteral-support requirements versus placebo. However, apraglutide remains investigational, is not FDA-approved, and a confirmatory Phase 3 trial is ongoing.
