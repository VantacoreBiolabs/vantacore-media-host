SEQUENCE IDENTITY VS SIMILARITY: EXACT MATCH VS CHEMICAL COUSIN.

• Sequence identity counts aligned positions with exactly the same amino acid.
• Sequence similarity can also count substitutions with related chemical properties.
• Similarity is usually evaluated with a scoring matrix, while both measures begin with an alignment.
• Reported percentages can change with the aligned region, gap handling, and scoring settings.

Evidence check: high identity or similarity can support a research hypothesis, but it does not by itself prove shared three-dimensional structure, biological function, or common ancestry.
