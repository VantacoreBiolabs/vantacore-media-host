BLANK SAMPLES: A BLANK ISN'T EMPTY WORK.

• A blank omits the target analyte or real sample while retaining selected parts of the test system.
• A method blank travels through the preparation and analytical workflow to check for contamination introduced along the way.
• A reagent blank focuses on contamination from reagents; a field blank checks what may be introduced during collection and handling.
• Unexpected blank signal can reveal background, contamination, or interference that could affect low-level measurements.

Evidence check: acceptable blank levels and corrective actions are method-specific. A clean blank reduces one source of doubt, but it does not prove that every sample result is accurate.
