PLERIXAFOR: NOT A PEPTIDE—A TWO-RING CXCR4 BLOCKER.

• Plerixafor is a synthetic bicyclam small molecule, not a peptide.
• Its structure contains two cyclam rings joined by a central linker.
• It antagonizes CXCR4, blocking the receptor's interaction with the signaling protein CXCL12.
• The CXCR4–CXCL12 pair helps retain blood-forming stem and progenitor cells in bone marrow; interrupting that signal increases their movement into circulation.

Evidence check: plerixafor is FDA-approved for a specific stem-cell collection setting alongside G-CSF. That regulated use does not establish a general “regeneration” benefit or support unsupervised use.
