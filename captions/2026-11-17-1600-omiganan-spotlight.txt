OMIGANAN: 12 AMINO ACIDS. FOUR TRYPTOPHANS.

Here is the quick read:

• Omiganan is a synthetic 12-amino-acid peptide with an amidated end.
• It was designed as an analog of indolicidin, an innate-defense peptide first isolated from bovine neutrophils.
• Its sequence contains four tryptophans plus several positively charged arginine and lysine residues.
• Laboratory studies connect its activity with interactions at microbial membranes, although the exact mechanism is not fully settled.

Evidence check: activity in a laboratory assay does not prove clinical benefit. A Phase 3 catheter study missed its primary efficacy endpoint, and later clinical findings have been limited or mixed.
