WHEN MORE LOOKS LIKE LESS: THE HIGH-DOSE HOOK EFFECT.

Some sandwich immunoassays can behave in a counterintuitive way:

• The target normally forms a bridge between capture and detection antibodies.
• More complete bridges usually create a stronger signal.
• At an extremely high target concentration, antibodies can become saturated separately.
• With fewer complete bridges, the signal may fall and the result can appear falsely low.

Evidence check: not every immunoassay shows a hook effect. The risk depends on the assay design, target, and concentration range. When appropriate, validated dilution checks can reveal an unexpectedly suppressed signal.
