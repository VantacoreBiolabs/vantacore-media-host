RELAMORELIN: FIVE RESIDUES, ONE GHRELIN RECEPTOR

• Relamorelin, also called RM-131, is a synthetic pentapeptide.
• It is an analog of ghrelin, a peptide involved in signaling through the ghrelin receptor.
• Relamorelin activates GHSR1a, the ghrelin receptor.
• It was designed as a more stable, potent ghrelin-receptor agonist and studied for gastrointestinal motility.

Evidence check: Relamorelin remains investigational. Multiple Phase 3 studies were terminated after the sponsor ended the development program for business reasons. That history does not establish clinical benefit.
