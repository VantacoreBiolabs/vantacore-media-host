PEGCETACOPLAN: TWO PEPTIDES, ONE COMPLEMENT TARGET

• Pegcetacoplan is a symmetrical molecule built from two identical 15-residue peptide portions.
• The two peptide arms are attached to opposite ends of a linear 40-kDa polyethylene glycol (PEG) chain.
• Its peptide portions bind complement protein C3 and its activation fragment C3b.
• That binding regulates C3 cleavage and the generation of downstream complement signals.

Safety limitation: C3 is part of host defense, not just a molecular target. Current FDA labeling carries a boxed warning for serious and life-threatening infections caused by encapsulated bacteria.
