ELCATONIN: A CALCITONIN LOOP — REBUILT.

Elcatonin is a synthetic, 31-residue analog of eel calcitonin.

• Its compact loop joins residues 1 and 7.
• Natural eel calcitonin uses a sulfur-containing disulfide bridge there.
• Elcatonin replaces that bridge with an α-aminosuberic-acid carbon link.
• Its reported molecular target is CALCR, the calcitonin receptor.

Evidence check: the structure is well defined, but much of the mechanistic evidence comes from cell and animal models. A shared receptor target does not make different calcitonin analogs interchangeable, and regulatory status is product- and region-specific.
