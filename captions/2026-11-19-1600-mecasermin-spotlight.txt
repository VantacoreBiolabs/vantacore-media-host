MECASERMIN: 70 RESIDUES. THREE INTERNAL BRIDGES.

Mecasermin is recombinant human insulin-like growth factor-1, or rhIGF-1.

• It is one chain containing 70 amino acids.
• Three intramolecular disulfide bridges help stabilize its fold.
• Its amino-acid sequence is identical to endogenous human IGF-1.
• It is produced using recombinant DNA technology in engineered E. coli.

Evidence boundary: Mecasermin's defined identity and regulatory evidence should not be generalized to modified IGF-1 analogs. Changes in sequence can affect binding, stability, and biological behavior, and regulatory status is specific to the product and indication.
