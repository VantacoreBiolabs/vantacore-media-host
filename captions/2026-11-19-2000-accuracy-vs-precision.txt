ACCURACY vs PRECISION: CLOSE TO CORRECT ISN'T THE SAME AS CONSISTENT.

These two quality terms answer different questions.

• Accuracy asks how close a result is to an accepted reference value.
• Precision asks how closely repeated measurements agree with one another.
• A method can be precise but consistently off target.
• It can also average near the target while individual results are widely scattered.

Evidence check: accuracy and precision are evaluated against predefined, method-specific criteria. A clean-looking cluster alone does not prove a method is fit for purpose across its full reportable range.
