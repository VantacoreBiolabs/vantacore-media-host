DASIGLUCAGON: 29 RESIDUES. 7 DESIGNED SUBSTITUTIONS.

Dasiglucagon is a synthetic peptide analog of human glucagon.

• It contains 29 amino acids—the same chain length as native glucagon.
• Seven amino-acid positions are substituted.
• Those substitutions were introduced to improve physical and chemical stability in aqueous solution.
• It activates glucagon receptors in the liver, supporting glycogen breakdown and glucose release.

Evidence boundary: dasiglucagon is not identical to native glucagon. Its sequence, stability data, receptor activity, and regulatory evidence are specific to this molecule and should not be generalized to every glucagon analog.
