ONE PEPTIDE. TWO RECEPTOR PATHWAYS.

Survodutide (BI 456906) is an investigational peptide designed to activate both the GLP-1 and glucagon receptors.

• It is a modified, 29-amino-acid peptide.
• One molecule is designed to engage two receptor pathways.
• A C18 fatty-acid side chain helps extend how long the peptide remains available for study.
• It is being evaluated in Phase 3 clinical programs.

Evidence boundary: investigational does not mean approved. Safety and effectiveness remain specific to each trial and indication, and results cannot predict an individual's outcome.
