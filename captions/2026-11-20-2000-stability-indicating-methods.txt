CAN YOUR METHOD SEE DEGRADATION?

A stability-indicating analytical method is built to detect relevant quality changes during storage—not merely produce a clean-looking result.

• It measures meaningful changes in a defined quality attribute.
• It distinguishes intact material from relevant degradation products and interferences.
• Stress-exposed, aged, or spiked samples can help challenge the method's specificity.
• The supporting evidence and acceptance criteria must match the method's intended purpose.

Evidence boundary: “stability-indicating” describes what the method can detect. It does not prove that a tested material is stable under every formulation, package, temperature, light, humidity, or time condition.
