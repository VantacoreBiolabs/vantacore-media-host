TROFINETIDE: a three-residue analog with an unresolved mechanism.

• Trofinetide is a synthetic analog of GPE—glycine-proline-glutamate.
• GPE is the amino-terminal tripeptide fragment of IGF-1.
• Trofinetide modifies the middle proline residue with a methyl group, creating a related but distinct molecule.
• The FDA approved trofinetide in 2023 for Rett syndrome in adults and children age 2 and older.

Evidence boundary: the current U.S. prescribing information states that the mechanism behind its therapeutic effects is unknown. Approval supports a specific product and indication; it does not make every proposed mechanism established.
