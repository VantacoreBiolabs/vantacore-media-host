SIGNAL PEPTIDE vs MATURE CHAIN: one routes; one remains.

• A signal peptide is a short sequence usually found at the N-terminus.
• It helps direct many newly made proteins into the secretory pathway.
• The signal peptide is usually removed at a cleavage site.
• The mature chain is the processed peptide or protein that remains—and it may undergo additional processing first.

Evidence boundary: sequence tools can predict signal peptides and cleavage sites, but a prediction is not the same as direct experimental proof. Rare signal sequences are not cleaved.
