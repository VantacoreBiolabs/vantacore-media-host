URODILATIN: the ANP core plus four.

• Urodilatin is a 32-amino-acid natriuretic peptide.
• It contains the same 28-residue core sequence as atrial natriuretic peptide, or ANP.
• Four additional residues extend its N-terminus.
• It was isolated from human urine, and early structural evidence pointed to distinct processing in kidney tubules.

Evidence boundary: sequence overlap does not make two peptides biologically identical. Different processing and tissue distribution can change where a peptide is found and how it behaves.
