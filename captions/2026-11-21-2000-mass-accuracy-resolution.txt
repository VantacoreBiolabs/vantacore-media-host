MASS ACCURACY vs MASS RESOLUTION: one is closeness; one is separation.

• Mass accuracy compares a measured mass with its reference value.
• Their difference is the mass error, often expressed as a small fractional error.
• Mass resolution describes whether nearby m/z peaks can be distinguished.
• Resolution values depend on how peak width or peak separation is defined and reported.

Evidence boundary: high resolution does not guarantee an accurate mass measurement, and high mass accuracy does not guarantee that neighboring peaks are separated. A method may need both.
