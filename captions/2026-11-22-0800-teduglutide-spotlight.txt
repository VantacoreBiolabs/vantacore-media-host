TEDUGLUTIDE: one amino-acid change at position 2.

• Teduglutide is a 33-amino-acid analog of human GLP-2.
• It keeps the same chain length as native GLP-2.
• Glycine replaces alanine at the second position from the N-terminus.
• This is why the analog is also written as [Gly²]-GLP-2.
• The substitution reduces cleavage by the enzyme DPP-4.

Evidence boundary: changing one residue can alter enzyme susceptibility, but it does not make every structural, biological, or clinical property identical to native GLP-2. This post explains molecular identity, not treatment guidance.
