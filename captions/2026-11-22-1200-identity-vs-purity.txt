IDENTITY vs PURITY: same sample, different question.

• Identity testing asks whether the intended material is present.
• A useful identity procedure should distinguish the target from closely related lookalikes.
• Purity testing asks what other material is present, such as impurities or related substances.
• A purity result may be reported as a measured amount or checked against a defined limit.

Evidence boundary: passing an identity test does not prove high purity, and a purity result alone may not establish identity. They answer different questions and can require different procedures.
