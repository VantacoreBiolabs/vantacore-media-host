VOSORITIDE: a CNP sequence with a two-residue extension.

• Vosoritide is a 39-amino-acid C-type natriuretic peptide analog.
• Its core contains the 37 C-terminal residues of human CNP53.
• Pro-Gly adds two residues at the N-terminus.
• One disulfide bridge links Cys23 and Cys39, closing a peptide ring.
• The N-terminal extension increases resistance to neutral endopeptidase degradation.

Evidence boundary: a two-residue extension can change enzyme susceptibility, but sequence alone does not establish every biological or clinical property. This is a molecular-identity spotlight, not treatment guidance.
