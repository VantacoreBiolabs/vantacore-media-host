PRALMORELIN: GHRP-2 in six residues.

• Pralmorelin is also known as GHRP-2 or KP-102.
• It is a synthetic hexapeptide—a chain built from six residues.
• Its sequence includes modified D-amino acids and ends in an amidated lysine.
• It activates the growth-hormone secretagogue receptor, also called the ghrelin receptor.

Evidence boundary: pralmorelin is used in Japan as a diagnostic stimulus for evaluating growth-hormone release. Receptor activity and diagnostic use do not establish broad treatment outcomes, and approval status varies by country.
