MONOISOTOPIC MASS vs AVERAGE MASS: same molecule, different isotope calculation.

• Monoisotopic mass uses the mass of the most abundant isotope of each element.
• It represents one defined isotope composition.
• Average mass is weighted for the molecule’s isotopic composition.
• That means the same molecular formula can have different reported mass values depending on the convention used.

Evidence boundary: both are calculated values, not automatically measured results. Software, isotope assumptions, and the reporting convention should be stated before comparing numbers.
