TEIXOBACTIN: AN 11-RESIDUE RING + TAIL

• Teixobactin is a natural cyclic depsipeptide built from 11 residues.
• Its last four residues close into a compact ring; the other seven form a linear tail.
• The ring includes enduracididine, an unusual non-proteinogenic amino acid.
• Laboratory studies show binding to lipid II and lipid III—building blocks used in bacterial cell-wall pathways.

Evidence boundary: the core evidence is laboratory and animal research, and development remains preclinical. Molecular mechanism does not establish human safety or effectiveness.
