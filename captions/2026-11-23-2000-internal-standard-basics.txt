INTERNAL STANDARDS: A REFERENCE SIGNAL IN EVERY SAMPLE

• An internal standard is added at a known, constant amount to calibration standards, quality controls, and study samples.
• It is usually a structurally similar compound or a stable-isotope-labeled version of the target.
• Because it travels through sample preparation and analysis with the target, its response can help reveal workflow variation.
• Quantification commonly compares the target response with the internal-standard response.

Evidence boundary: an internal standard can help correct or flag some variability, but it cannot automatically fix interference, poor calibration, matrix effects, or an unsuitable method. Its performance must be validated for the specific workflow.
