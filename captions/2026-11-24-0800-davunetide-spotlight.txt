DAVUNETIDE: EIGHT RESIDUES FROM A MUCH LARGER PROTEIN

• Davunetide is also called NAP.
• Its sequence is NAPVSIPQ—a chain of eight amino acids.
• The sequence comes from a small region within activity-dependent neuroprotective protein, or ADNP.
• Laboratory research links its SIP motif with microtubule end-binding proteins and microtubule-associated processes.

Evidence boundary: human trials have been conducted, but a randomized phase 2/3 study in progressive supranuclear palsy found no benefit over placebo on its primary measures. That result does not establish effectiveness for PSP or other conditions.
