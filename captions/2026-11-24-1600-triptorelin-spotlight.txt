TRIPTORELIN: 10 RESIDUES, ONE STANDOUT POSITION

• Triptorelin is a synthetic 10-residue analog of gonadotropin-releasing hormone, or GnRH.
• Its sequence is pGlu–His–Trp–Ser–Tyr–D-Trp–Leu–Arg–Pro–Gly-NH₂.
• Position 6 contains D-tryptophan instead of the glycine found in native GnRH.
• Its N-terminus is pyroglutamate, and its C-terminus is glycinamide.
• It acts as an agonist at the GnRH receptor.

Evidence boundary: approved medicines contain triptorelin in specific formulations and indications. Molecular identity and receptor activity do not make products interchangeable, establish every outcome, or provide dosing or administration guidance.
