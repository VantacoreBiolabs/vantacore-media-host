GONADORELIN: THE NATIVE-SEQUENCE GnRH

• Gonadorelin is a decapeptide—a chain of 10 amino acids.
• Its sequence is pGlu–His–Trp–Ser–Tyr–Gly–Leu–Arg–Pro–Gly–NH₂.
• That amino-acid order matches naturally occurring gonadotropin-releasing hormone, or GnRH.
• GnRH is produced by the hypothalamus and signals the anterior pituitary to release LH and FSH.

Evidence boundary: Matching the native hormone’s sequence and signaling pathway does not make every product or salt form interchangeable, establish every clinical use, or provide administration guidance. Formulation, indication, regulatory status, and evidence still matter.
