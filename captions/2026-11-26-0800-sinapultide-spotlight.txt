SINAPULTIDE: 21 RESIDUES, ONLY TWO LETTERS

• Sinapultide, also called KL4, is a synthetic peptide made from 21 amino acids.
• Its sequence is KLLLLKLLLLKLLLLKLLLLK—using only lysine (K) and leucine (L).
• The design repeats one lysine followed by four leucines and was created to mimic key features of human surfactant protein B.
• Laboratory studies find that KL4 forms a largely helical structure in lipid environments and interacts with phospholipid membranes.

Evidence boundary: Clinical studies evaluated lucinactant, a multi-component formulation containing sinapultide plus phospholipids and palmitic acid—not sinapultide by itself. Laboratory studies also show that KL4’s orientation and behavior can depend on the surrounding lipid environment.
