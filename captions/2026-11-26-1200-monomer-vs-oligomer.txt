MONOMER vs OLIGOMER: one unit or a small assembly?

• A monomer is one protein chain or subunit acting as a single unit.
• An oligomer is a small assembly made from multiple subunits.
• Dimer, trimer, and tetramer mean 2, 3, and 4 subunits.
• Homo-oligomers use matching subunits; hetero-oligomers use different ones.

Evidence limit: a crystal structure can suggest an assembly, but crystal packing alone may not prove which form is biologically relevant in solution. Researchers compare structural and solution-based evidence.
