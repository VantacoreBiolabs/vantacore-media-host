NESFATIN-1: 82 residues from a larger precursor.

• Nesfatin-1 is a polypeptide made from 82 amino acids.
• It corresponds to the N-terminal region of nucleobindin-2, or NUCB2.
• It was first described in 2006 through research involving the rat hypothalamus.
• Early studies examined its role in food-intake signaling in rodents.

Evidence limit: the receptor responsible for nesfatin-1 signaling remains unresolved, and much of the mechanistic evidence is preclinical. Molecular identity and animal findings do not establish a human treatment or outcome.
