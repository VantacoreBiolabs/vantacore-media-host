LIGAND vs RECEPTOR: the binding partner and the target.

• A ligand is a molecule that binds to a biological target.
• A receptor is a target protein that can detect binding and change its activity.
• An agonist is a ligand that binds and activates a receptor response.
• An antagonist reduces the action of an agonist—often by interfering with receptor signaling.

Evidence limit: binding alone does not prove activation, selectivity, or a meaningful outcome. The receptor, ligand, concentration, pathway, and experimental system all affect what happens next.
