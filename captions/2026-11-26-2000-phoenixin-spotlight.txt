PHOENIXIN: one precursor, two main peptide forms.

• Phoenixin-14 and phoenixin-20 contain 14 and 20 amino acids.
• Both correspond to sequences within the small integral membrane protein 20, or SMIM20.
• Their main forms carry a C-terminal amide modification.
• Phoenixin was identified in 2013 using bioinformatics followed by peptide purification and analysis.

Evidence limit: most functional findings come from cell and animal studies. Its processing, signaling pathways, and relevance in humans remain active research questions—not established treatment claims.
