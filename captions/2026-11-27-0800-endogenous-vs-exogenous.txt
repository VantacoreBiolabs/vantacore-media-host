ENDOGENOUS vs EXOGENOUS: MADE INSIDE vs COMES FROM OUTSIDE

• Endogenous means produced within an organism, cell, or biological system.
• Exogenous means originating outside and being introduced to the system.
• The same or a similar molecule can be studied in either context.
• The label describes source—not dose, exposure, effect, safety, effectiveness, or approval.

Evidence note: Origin is only one part of interpretation. Molecular form, concentration or exposure, timing, route, and the experimental system can all change results.
