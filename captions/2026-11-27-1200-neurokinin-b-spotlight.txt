NEUROKININ B: 10 RESIDUES FROM A 121-RESIDUE PRECURSOR

• Neurokinin B is a mature peptide made from 10 amino acids.
• It is cleaved from the much larger 121-residue TAC3 precursor.
• Its sequence ends with an amide modification—a common feature in the tachykinin family.
• It is the preferred endogenous agonist at the neurokinin-3 receptor, also called NK3R.

Evidence note: Molecular identity, receptor signaling, and genetic associations help researchers map biology, but they do not by themselves establish clinical safety, effectiveness, or an approved use.
