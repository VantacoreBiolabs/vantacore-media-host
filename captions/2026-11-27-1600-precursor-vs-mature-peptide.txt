PRECURSOR vs MATURE PEPTIDE: STARTING CHAIN vs FINISHED SIGNAL

• A precursor is a larger starting protein or peptide chain made by the cell.
• Processing enzymes can cut that chain at specific sites.
• The mature peptide is a released product that may be shorter and chemically modified.
• One precursor can produce multiple peptide products—sometimes in tissue-specific patterns.

Evidence note: A predicted sequence or possible cleavage site does not prove which mature products are actually formed, how abundant they are, or whether they are biologically active in a given system.
