NEUROTENSIN: 13 RESIDUES WITH A 6-RESIDUE BINDING CORE

• Human neurotensin is a short, linear peptide made from 13 amino acids.
• It is cut from a much larger 170-residue precursor encoded by the NTS gene.
• That same precursor also produces another peptide called neuromedin N.
• The final six residues of neurotensin form the primary high-affinity binding and activation segment at the NTS1 receptor.

Evidence note: Receptor structures and short-fragment experiments help map molecular interactions, but they do not by themselves establish whole-body effects, clinical safety, effectiveness, or an approved use. Tissue-specific processing can also change which products are present.
