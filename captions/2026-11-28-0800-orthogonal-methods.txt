ORTHOGONAL METHODS: DIFFERENT PRINCIPLES, ONE STRONGER CONCLUSION

• Orthogonal methods examine the same property in the same sample.
• They rely on different physical measurement principles, so they do not fail in exactly the same way.
• When results agree, confidence rises because one method-specific bias or interference is less likely to explain both findings.
• When results disagree, that is a reason to investigate—not a result to hide.

Evidence note: Agreement between different methods strengthens a conclusion, but it does not prove every quality attribute. Each procedure still needs to be fit for its intended purpose and well characterized. For peptide testing, identity, purity, quantity, sterility, and biological activity are separate questions.
