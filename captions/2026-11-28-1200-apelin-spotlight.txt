APELIN: ONE PRECURSOR, SEVERAL ACTIVE FORMS

• The human APLN gene encodes a 77-amino-acid peptide precursor.
• Proteolytic processing releases shorter peptides from its C-terminal region.
• Well-known endogenous forms include apelin-36, apelin-17, apelin-13, and pyroglutamated apelin-13.
• These forms act as endogenous agonists at the apelin receptor, also called APLNR or APJ.

Evidence note: The exact processing pathway, form abundance, and measured signaling can vary by tissue and experimental system. Molecular identity and receptor activity do not by themselves establish clinical safety, effectiveness, dosing, or an approved use.
