Liraglutide: one peptide, a longer signal.

It is an acylated GLP-1 analog with 97% amino-acid sequence similarity to human GLP-1. A fatty-acid side chain helps it self-associate and bind albumin, slowing absorption and protecting the signal from rapid enzymatic breakdown.

At the GLP-1 receptor, liraglutide supports glucose-dependent insulin release, lowers glucagon when glucose is elevated, and delays gastric emptying.

The important context: liraglutide is a prescription compound. Evidence, approved uses, contraindications, and risks are product-specific—including serious gastrointestinal warnings and a boxed thyroid C-cell tumor warning based on rodent findings whose relevance to humans is unknown. This post is education, not medical advice.
