PUBLICATION BIAS: WHAT IF THE MISSING STUDIES TELL A DIFFERENT STORY?

• Positive or striking results may be easier to publish.
• Null or negative findings can stay unseen.
• That can make the published evidence look more favorable than the full research record.
• Trial registration and results reporting help make the evidence base more complete.

Evidence note: funnel plots and other checks can flag possible missing evidence, but they cannot prove publication bias on their own.
