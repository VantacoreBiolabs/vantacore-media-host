ABALOPARATIDE: A PEPTIDE SIGNAL FOR BONE BUILDING 🦴

Abaloparatide is a synthetic 34-amino-acid analog of human parathyroid hormone-related peptide, PTHrP(1-34).

• It acts as an agonist at the PTH1 receptor.
• Receptor activation turns on the cAMP signaling pathway.
• The signal stimulates osteoblast activity—the cells that build bone.
• New bone formation occurs on trabecular and cortical bone surfaces.

Evidence check: abaloparatide is a prescription medicine with specific high-risk-osteoporosis indications. Label warnings include orthostatic hypotension, hypercalcemia and hypercalciuria, plus an osteosarcoma signal observed in rats whose relevance to humans is unknown.
