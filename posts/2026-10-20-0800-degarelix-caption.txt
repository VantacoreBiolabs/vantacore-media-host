DEGARELIX: BLOCKING THE SIGNAL AT ITS SOURCE

Degarelix is a synthetic linear decapeptide—a chain of 10 amino-acid building blocks designed to antagonize the GnRH receptor.

• It binds reversibly to GnRH receptors in the pituitary.
• Receptor blockade reduces release of LH and FSH.
• Lower gonadotropin signaling reduces downstream testosterone production.
• As an antagonist, it suppresses the pathway directly rather than first stimulating it.

Evidence check: degarelix is a prescription medicine for advanced prostate cancer—not a general hormone-management peptide. Serious hypersensitivity reactions have been reported, and androgen-deprivation therapy may prolong the QT interval.
