PREREGISTRATION: DECIDE BEFORE YOU SEE THE RESULTS.

Preregistration creates a time-stamped record of a study plan before data collection or analysis.

• Write the research question first.
• Specify methods, variables, and the analysis plan.
• Separate planned tests from later exploration.
• If the plan changes, document what changed and why.

Important limit: preregistration improves transparency, but it cannot fix weak methods, poor measurements, bias, or missing data.
