OCTREOTIDE: A PEPTIDE SIGNAL BRAKE

Octreotide is a synthetic cyclic octapeptide designed to act like somatostatin—one of the body’s natural signal-limiting hormones.

• It contains eight amino-acid building blocks.
• Its actions resemble natural somatostatin.
• It suppresses release of growth hormone, glucagon and insulin.
• It also affects several endocrine and digestive signaling pathways.

Evidence check: octreotide is a prescription medicine with specific approved uses—not a general hormone-balancing peptide. Important risks include gallstones or biliary complications, changes in blood glucose, and slowed heart rate or conduction abnormalities.
