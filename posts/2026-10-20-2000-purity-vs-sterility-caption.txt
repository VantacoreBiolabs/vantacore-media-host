PURITY ≠ STERILITY: SAME SAMPLE, DIFFERENT QUESTIONS.

A strong-looking purity percentage answers only one part of a quality check.

• Purity testing examines the target material and detected chemical impurities.
• Sterility testing asks whether viable microorganisms are detected under defined test conditions.
• Bacterial endotoxin requires a separate test.
• Identity, strength, particulate matter and fill accuracy are separate quality attributes too.

Evidence check: no single result proves complete product quality. A high purity percentage does not establish sterility, confirm identity, verify strength, or rule out endotoxin.
