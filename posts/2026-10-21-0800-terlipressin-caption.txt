TERLIPRESSIN: A VASOPRESSIN SIGNAL WITH A PRODRUG TWIST

Terlipressin is a synthetic 12-amino-acid vasopressin analog.

• It favors vasopressin V1 receptors over V2 receptors.
• It has pharmacologic activity of its own.
• Tissue peptidases remove its first three glycine residues.
• That process releases active lysine vasopressin.
• V1 signaling constricts blood vessels and changes circulatory pressure.

Evidence check: terlipressin is a prescription hospital medicine approved for adults with hepatorenal syndrome involving rapidly worsening kidney function. Its label carries a boxed warning for serious or fatal respiratory failure, and it can cause ischemic events.
