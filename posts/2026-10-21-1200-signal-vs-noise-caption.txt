SIGNAL vs NOISE: CAN YOU SEE THE REAL PATTERN?

In research, the signal is the pattern you want to measure. Noise is the background variation that can hide or imitate it.

• A higher signal-to-noise ratio makes a pattern easier to distinguish.
• Noise can come from random fluctuation, the measurement system, or changing test conditions.
• Repeated measurements can reveal how consistent a pattern is.
• Controls and calibration help show what the background looks like.

Reality check: a clear signal does not prove identity, causation, or freedom from bias. There is also no single SNR formula used in every field, so methods must be compared carefully.
