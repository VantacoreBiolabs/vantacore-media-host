CORTICORELIN: A PEPTIDE THAT TESTS A HORMONE RELAY

Corticorelin ovine is a synthetic diagnostic peptide modeled on corticotropin-releasing hormone.

• It contains 41 amino acids.
• Its sequence matches ovine CRH and is similar to human CRH.
• It stimulates the anterior pituitary to release ACTH.
• ACTH then signals the adrenal cortex to produce cortisol.
• Measuring that response can help distinguish pituitary from ectopic ACTH production.

Evidence check: this is a prescription diagnostic peptide—not a wellness or performance compound. Responses vary, and hypersensitivity reactions can occur.
