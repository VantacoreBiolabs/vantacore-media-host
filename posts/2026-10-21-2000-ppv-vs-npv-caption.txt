PPV vs NPV: WHAT DOES YOUR TEST RESULT MEAN?

Predictive values start with the result you received.

• PPV asks: given a positive result, how often is the condition really present?
• NPV asks: given a negative result, how often is the condition really absent?
• PPV and NPV are different from sensitivity and specificity.
• Predictive values change when prevalence changes.
• The same test can therefore perform differently across populations.

Reality check: a test result is not certainty. Interpretation depends on the test, the population being tested, and the real-world chance that the condition is present.
