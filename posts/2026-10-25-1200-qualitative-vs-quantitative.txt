QUALITATIVE ≠ QUANTITATIVE

Qualitative data helps researchers understand meaning, context and experience through words, images and observations.

Quantitative data uses numbers, counts and measured values to describe patterns or test relationships.

Neither approach is automatically stronger. The best fit depends on the research question, sampling, collection and analysis—and many studies use both.
