METRELEPTIN: A LEPTIN SIGNAL REPLACEMENT

Metreleptin is a 147-amino-acid recombinant analog of human leptin. It differs from natural human leptin by one added methionine at its amino end.

It binds and activates the leptin receptor, which signals through the JAK/STAT pathway.

Important boundary: in the U.S., metreleptin is approved only as an adjunct to diet for complications of leptin deficiency in congenital or acquired generalized lipodystrophy. It is not a general weight-loss compound.

Evidence check: current labeling carries a boxed warning for neutralizing antibodies and lymphoma risk, and access is restricted through a REMS.
