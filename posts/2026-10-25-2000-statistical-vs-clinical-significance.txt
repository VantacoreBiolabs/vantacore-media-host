SIGNIFICANT ≠ IMPORTANT

Statistical significance asks whether the observed data conflict with a no-effect model at a chosen threshold.

Clinical significance asks a different question: is the effect large enough to matter in real practice?

A p-value does not tell you the size of an effect. To interpret a result well, also look at the effect size, confidence interval, potential benefits, potential harms and context.

Evidence check: with a very large sample, even a tiny and practically unimportant effect can become statistically significant.
