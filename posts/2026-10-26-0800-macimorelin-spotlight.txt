MACIMORELIN: A DIAGNOSTIC SIGNAL—NOT GH THERAPY

Macimorelin is a synthetic growth-hormone secretagogue receptor agonist. It acts as a peptidomimetic ghrelin-receptor agonist and briefly stimulates growth-hormone release so the response can be measured.

Its FDA-approved role is diagnostic: helping evaluate growth-hormone deficiency in adults. It is not growth-hormone replacement therapy.

Evidence check: test performance has not been established for people with BMI above 40, and medicines that affect GH release can alter test accuracy.
