PARALLEL ≠ CROSSOVER

Parallel-group trials compare different groups receiving different study conditions. Each participant usually remains in one assigned group.

Crossover trials use the same participants across multiple conditions in sequence, allowing each person to serve as their own comparison.

A washout period may be used between conditions—but it cannot always remove lingering effects.

Evidence check: crossover designs can be misleading when carryover is likely, the condition changes over time, or the intervention has lasting effects.
