GANIRELIX: THE GnRH BRAKE

Ganirelix is a synthetic 10-amino-acid peptide and a GnRH antagonist.

It blocks GnRH receptors in the pituitary instead of activating them.

That reduces the release of luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH).

Its labeled role is preventing a premature LH surge during controlled ovarian stimulation.

Evidence check: ganirelix is a prescription fertility medicine. Serious allergic reactions—including anaphylaxis—have been reported, and it is contraindicated during pregnancy.
