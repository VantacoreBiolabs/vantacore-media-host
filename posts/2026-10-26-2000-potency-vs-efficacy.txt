POTENCY ≠ EFFICACY

Potency asks how much of a compound is needed to produce a specified effect.

A more potent compound can reach the same effect at a lower concentration.

Efficacy asks about the greatest effect a compound can produce.

Higher potency does not automatically mean a higher maximum effect.

Evidence check: valid comparisons need the same outcome and test system. Laboratory potency alone does not establish real-world benefit, safety or clinical usefulness.
