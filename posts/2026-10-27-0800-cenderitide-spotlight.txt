CENDERITIDE: ONE PEPTIDE, TWO RECEPTORS

Cenderitide is an engineered 37-amino-acid natriuretic peptide.

It combines the 22-amino-acid human C-type natriuretic peptide with a 15-amino-acid tail from Dendroaspis natriuretic peptide.

It was designed to activate both GC-A and GC-B receptors.

Both receptor pathways generate the second messenger cGMP.

Evidence check: cenderitide remains investigational, not an established therapy. Human findings come from small, early studies and require confirmation in larger trials.
