HAZARD RATIO ≠ PROBABILITY

A hazard ratio compares the event rate over time between two groups.

HR = 1 means the event rates are similar over the observed period.

HR below 1 means the first group has a lower event rate; HR above 1 means it has a higher event rate.

It does not directly tell you the chance that an event will happen, or the absolute difference between groups.

Evidence check: read the 95% confidence interval and check whether the proportional-hazards assumption is reasonable. One number can hide changes over time.
