NAFARELIN: SIGNAL ON—THEN DOWN

Nafarelin is a synthetic 10-amino-acid analog of gonadotropin-releasing hormone, or GnRH.

As a GnRH agonist, it initially stimulates the pituitary to release LH and FSH.

With continued signaling, pituitary GnRH receptors become less responsive.

The result is reduced gonadotropin output and lower downstream gonadal-steroid production.

Evidence check: activation and suppression are different phases of the same agonist mechanism. Nafarelin is a prescription medicine with important warnings and is not a general hormone-support compound.
