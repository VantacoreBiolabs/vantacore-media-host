STERILE ≠ ENDOTOXIN-FREE

Sterility testing looks for viable microorganisms that can grow under the test conditions.

Bacterial endotoxin testing looks for toxic cell-wall components from Gram-negative bacteria.

A process can kill bacteria without reliably removing the endotoxin they left behind.

That means a sterility result and an endotoxin result answer different safety questions.

Evidence check: one passing test cannot substitute for the other. Neither result alone establishes identity, purity, potency, or overall product quality.
