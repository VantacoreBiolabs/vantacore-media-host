DESIRUDIN: THE 1-TO-1 THROMBIN LOCK

Desirudin is a recombinant 65-amino-acid peptide modeled after hirudin, a naturally occurring anticoagulant protein.

It directly binds both free-circulating and clot-bound thrombin.

One desirudin molecule binds one thrombin molecule and blocks thrombin-driven clotting activity.

Unlike indirect anticoagulants, it does not need antithrombin as a helper protein.

Evidence check: direct thrombin inhibition also creates serious bleeding risk. Desirudin was FDA-approved for clot prevention after hip replacement, but the FDA currently lists the product as discontinued.
