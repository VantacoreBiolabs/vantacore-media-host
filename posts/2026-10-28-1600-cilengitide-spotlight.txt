CILENGITIDE: THE 5-AMINO-ACID INTEGRIN LOCK

• Cilengitide is a cyclic peptide made from five amino acids.
• Its RGD motif is designed to engage integrins—cell-surface proteins that help cells interact with their surroundings.
• It selectively targets the αvβ3 and αvβ5 integrins.
• Researchers studied it in several cancers, including glioblastoma.

Evidence check: Early studies looked promising, but the large CENTRIC Phase 3 trial found no survival improvement when cilengitide was added to standard treatment. Its anticancer development was discontinued—a useful reminder that a strong mechanism does not guarantee clinical benefit.
