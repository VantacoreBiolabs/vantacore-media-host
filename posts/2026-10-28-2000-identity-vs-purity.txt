IDENTITY ≠ PURITY

• Identity asks whether the sample contains the substance it claims to contain.
• Purity looks at unwanted material or impurities in the sample.
• Assay is another question: how much of the target analyte is present?
• A high purity percentage does not automatically prove the compound's identity.

Evidence check: FDA guidance treats identification, assay and impurity testing as separate analytical categories. Strong identity testing compares a sample with a reference standard and may require complementary methods—not just one retention time.
