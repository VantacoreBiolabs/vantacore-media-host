LARAZOTIDE: THE 8-AMINO-ACID TIGHT-JUNCTION MODULATOR

• Larazotide acetate is a synthetic, single-chain peptide made from eight amino acids.
• Researchers designed it to influence tight junctions—the connections between neighboring intestinal cells.
• Its proposed mechanism centers on limiting abnormal increases in intestinal-barrier permeability.
• It was studied as an investigational compound in celiac disease.

Evidence check: Earlier studies produced mixed signals. In 2022, the Phase 3 CeDLara study stopped after a planned interim analysis showed that an impractically large enrollment would be needed to demonstrate a significant outcome. Development for celiac disease was discontinued, and larazotide is not FDA-approved.
