Aviptadil: the 28-amino-acid VIP.

• Aviptadil is a synthetic version of vasoactive intestinal peptide, often shortened to VIP.
• VIP is a chain of 28 amino acids.
• Researchers have studied its signaling in the lungs and blood vessels.
• Aviptadil has reached human trials, including studies in respiratory illness.

Evidence check: trial results have been mixed, and FDA orphan designation is not the same as FDA approval.
