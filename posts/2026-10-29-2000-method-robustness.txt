Robustness: can the method handle small changes?

• Researchers deliberately vary a test condition.
• Examples can include temperature, timing or pH.
• They check whether the method still meets its expected performance limits.
• The focus is on changes most likely to matter during normal use.

Evidence check: robustness supports confidence in normal-use reliability, but it does not prove that every result is correct or replace the method’s other validation checks.
