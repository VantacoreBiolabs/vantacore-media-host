Pexiganan: the 22-amino-acid membrane disruptor.

• Pexiganan is a synthetic antimicrobial peptide.
• It is built from 22 amino acids.
• Its design was inspired by magainin 2, a host-defense peptide first identified in frog skin.
• Researchers studied it as a topical compound that can disturb microbial membranes.

Evidence check: earlier active-comparator trials produced encouraging signals, but later placebo-controlled Phase 3 studies did not establish superiority. Research status is not approval.
