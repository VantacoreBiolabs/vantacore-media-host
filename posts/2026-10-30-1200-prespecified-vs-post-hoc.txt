Prespecified vs. post hoc: when was the analysis chosen?

• Prespecified analyses are planned before study results are examined.
• Outcomes, groups and statistical methods are defined in advance.
• Post-hoc analyses are selected after results become available.
• Post-hoc work can reveal patterns worth testing in future research.

Evidence check: post-hoc findings can generate useful hypotheses, but they face a higher false-positive risk and usually need confirmation in new data.
