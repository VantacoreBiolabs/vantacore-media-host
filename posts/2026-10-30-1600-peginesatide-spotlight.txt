Peginesatide: the PEG-linked EPO-receptor signal.

• Peginesatide is a synthetic peptide dimer.
• The dimer is linked to a polyethylene glycol chain.
• It was engineered to activate the erythropoietin receptor.
• Its peptide sequence is unrelated to natural erythropoietin.

Evidence check: the FDA approved peginesatide in 2012, but it was voluntarily withdrawn from the market less than a year after launch following serious hypersensitivity reports, including fatal anaphylaxis cases.
