Batch effects: when the run looks like the result.

• Batch effects are technical differences—not the biology being studied.
• They can come from the processing day, reagent lot, operator or instrument.
• They may hide a real signal or create a false-looking pattern.
• Good design spreads comparison groups across every batch.

Evidence check: statistical correction can help when batches overlap, but it cannot reliably rescue a study where group and batch are perfectly confounded.
