Reltecimod: the CD28 signal modulator.

• Reltecimod is a synthetic immune-modulating peptide.
• Its design mimics part of the CD28 receptor interface.
• It was developed to dampen excessive T-cell stimulation triggered by bacterial superantigens.
• Researchers studied it in severe-infection settings.

Evidence check: in a Phase 3 trial, the primary composite endpoint was not statistically significant in the modified intent-to-treat analysis. FDA orphan designation is not approval.
