Daptomycin: the membrane depolarizer.

• Daptomycin is a cyclic lipopeptide antibacterial.
• Calcium helps it bind to Gram-positive bacterial membranes.
• That interaction rapidly collapses the membrane's electrical potential.
• Loss of membrane potential disrupts essential cellular processes.

Evidence check: pulmonary surfactant inhibits daptomycin's antibacterial activity. Its FDA labeling specifically states that it is not indicated for pneumonia.
