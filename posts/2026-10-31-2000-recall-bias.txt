Recall bias: when memory bends the data.

• Recall bias can appear when research relies on participants remembering past events.
• Events may be forgotten, misdated or reported without important details.
• Comparison groups may remember or report the same past exposure differently.
• Objective records and shorter recall windows can reduce reliance on memory.

Evidence check: recall bias can inflate or hide an association. It is not automatic in every retrospective study—reliable historical records can reduce the problem.
