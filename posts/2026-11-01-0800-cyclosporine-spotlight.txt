Cyclosporine: the 11-amino-acid signal brake.

• Cyclosporine is an 11-amino-acid cyclic polypeptide.
• It was originally produced as a metabolite by a fungus.
• Inside cells, cyclosporine first binds a protein called cyclophilin.
• The cyclosporine–cyclophilin complex then inhibits calcineurin signaling.

Evidence check: modified and non-modified cyclosporine formulations are not bioequivalent or automatically interchangeable. Formulation names matter when interpreting research and labeling.
