Cross-sectional vs. longitudinal: snapshot vs. movie.

• Cross-sectional studies measure exposures and outcomes at one point in time.
• They are useful for estimating how common something is right now—its prevalence.
• Longitudinal studies observe participants repeatedly across time.
• They can track change, sequence and new events—incidence.

Evidence check: observing timing helps establish which event came first, but observational follow-up alone does not prove causation.
