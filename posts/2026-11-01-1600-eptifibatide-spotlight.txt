EPTIFIBATIDE: THE PLATELET-BRIDGE BLOCKER

• Eptifibatide is a small cyclic heptapeptide.
• Its design was inspired by barbourin, a platelet-receptor-blocking peptide found in rattlesnake venom.
• It targets GP IIb/IIIa receptors on platelets.
• By reversibly blocking fibrinogen and other adhesive ligands from binding, it interrupts the bridges that help platelets aggregate.

Evidence check: blocking platelet aggregation also raises bleeding risk. Current labeling identifies bleeding as the most common complication and also warns that thrombocytopenia can occur. This is mechanism education—not medical advice or administration guidance.
