PILOT STUDY ≠ PROOF IT WORKS

• A pilot study is a small-scale test of methods and procedures planned for a larger study.
• Its main question is practical: can the full study be done as intended?
• Researchers may check recruitment, retention, randomization, data collection and follow-up.
• Feasibility goals and success criteria should be defined in advance.

Evidence check: pilot studies are generally designed to assess feasibility—not to confirm effectiveness. Small samples can produce highly uncertain effect estimates, so an encouraging pilot result still needs an appropriately designed definitive study.
