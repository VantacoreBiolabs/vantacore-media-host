GLATIRAMER ACETATE: NOT ONE PEPTIDE—A PEPTIDE MIXTURE

• Glatiramer acetate is a complex mixture of synthetic polypeptides.
• Its chains are built from four naturally occurring amino acids: glutamic acid, alanine, tyrosine and lysine.
• FDA chemistry reviews describe polypeptides roughly 40–100 amino acids long with non-uniform sequences.
• Its exact mechanism in humans is not fully understood; labeling describes immune-process modification as the leading explanation.

Evidence check: glatiramer acetate is not one identical molecular sequence. Because it is a heterogeneous peptide mixture, tightly controlled composition and manufacturing are central to product consistency.
