PENTAGASTRIN: THE FIVE-RESIDUE GASTRIN MIMIC

• Pentagastrin is a synthetic pentapeptide—a chain built around five amino-acid residues.
• It mimics key actions of the natural signaling hormone gastrin.
• Its signal stimulates gastric-acid secretion, along with pepsin and intrinsic factor.
• Historically, it was used as a diagnostic aid to test stomach-acid output.

Evidence check: the former U.S. product was discontinued from marketing, but FDA determined that withdrawal was not for safety or effectiveness reasons. Historical testing use does not establish every modern claimed use, and orphan designation does not equal approval.
