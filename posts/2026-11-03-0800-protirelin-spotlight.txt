PROTIRELIN: THE 3-AMINO-ACID HORMONE RELAY

Protirelin is the synthetic form of thyrotropin-releasing hormone (TRH)—a tiny peptide with a big signaling job.

• It is a tripeptide: just three amino-acid residues.
• Natural TRH is released by the hypothalamus.
• Its signal prompts the anterior pituitary to release thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) and prolactin.
• Synthetic TRH was historically used as a diagnostic probe of pituitary response.

Evidence limit: response patterns vary with physiology, medications, and clinical context. A TRH response is not a standalone diagnosis, and modern sensitive TSH testing has reduced the test’s routine role.
