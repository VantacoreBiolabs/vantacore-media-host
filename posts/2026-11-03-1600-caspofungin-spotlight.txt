CASPOFUNGIN: THE FUNGAL CELL-WALL BLOCKER

Caspofungin is a semisynthetic cyclic lipopeptide in the echinocandin family.

• It was developed from a fungal fermentation product.
• It blocks synthesis of β(1,3)-D-glucan, a key building material in susceptible fungal cell walls.
• That glucan is not found in mammalian cells.
• Its target is glucan synthase, a membrane enzyme that helps assemble the wall.

Evidence limit: activity varies by fungal species, and changes in FKS glucan-synthase subunits can reduce susceptibility. A molecular target alone does not predict every organism’s response.
