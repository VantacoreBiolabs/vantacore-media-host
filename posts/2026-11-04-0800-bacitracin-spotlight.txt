BACITRACIN: THE LIPID-CARRIER TRAP

Bacitracin is not one molecule—it is a mixture of related cyclic polypeptides, with bacitracin A as the major component.

• The mixture is produced through Bacillus fermentation.
• Bacterial cell-wall building blocks ride a recyclable membrane lipid carrier.
• Bacitracin binds the carrier’s pyrophosphate form.
• That trap interferes with carrier recycling and peptidoglycan assembly.

Evidence limit: bacitracin composition and bacterial susceptibility can vary. Knowing the mechanism does not predict every strain’s response.
